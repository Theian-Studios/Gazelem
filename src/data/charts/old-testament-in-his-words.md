# Old Testament Quotations in the Words of Jesus
* subtitle: What He cited, where it came from, and what He did with it
* volume: new-testament
* order: 9
* columns: Jesus’ Words | OT Source | Note on the Citation | Reference (KJV)

Every passage Jesus quotes or unmistakably invokes from the Hebrew scriptures, with its source and a note on how He handles the text — where He follows the Hebrew, where the Septuagint, where He fuses two texts into one, and where He stops a quotation short. Deuteronomy answers the tempter; Hosea answers the Pharisees; the Psalms of the righteous sufferer become His own dying voice.

31 citations in 4 groups. The list holds to explicit quotations and direct invocations in His own words; the Gospel writers’ own fulfillment citations (“that it might be fulfilled”) are a separate, larger corpus. Allusions without quotation — Noah, Lot, the brasen serpent, manna — would extend the chart further and are candidates for a companion table.

## The Wilderness Temptations
Three answers, all from Deuteronomy — the wilderness book answering the wilderness test.

- “It is written, Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of God” | Deut 8:3 | Israel’s manna lesson turned against the tempter | Matt 4:4 (Lk 4:4)
- “It is written again, Thou shalt not tempt the Lord thy God” | Deut 6:16 | Answering a temptation that itself quoted scripture (Ps 91) | Matt 4:7 (Lk 4:12)
- “It is written, Thou shalt worship the Lord thy God, and him only shalt thou serve” | Deut 6:13 | “Only” sharpens the source’s “and shalt swear by his name” | Matt 4:10 (Lk 4:8)

## Announcing His Mission
The texts He chose to define His errand.

- “The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor...” | Isa 61:1–2 | Read at Nazareth; He stops mid-verse, before “the day of vengeance” — an editorial act: this coming is for the acceptable year | Luke 4:18–19
- “I will have mercy, and not sacrifice” | Hos 6:6 | Quoted twice — for eating with sinners, and for the sabbath grainfields: “go ye and learn what that meaneth” | Matt 9:13; 12:7
- “As Jonas was three days and three nights in the whale’s belly...” | Jonah 1:17 | The only sign given “this generation” — read as a type of the burial and rising | Matt 12:39–40 (Lk 11:29–30)
- “It is written in the prophets, And they shall be all taught of God” | Isa 54:13 | Grounding the doctrine that the Father draws souls to Him | John 6:45
- “Behold, I send my messenger before thy face...” | Mal 3:1 | Applied to John the Baptist — with the pronoun turned: Malachi’s “before me” becomes “before thee” | Matt 11:10 (Lk 7:27)

## Teaching and Controversy
Scripture wielded in dispute — “Have ye not read?”

- “This people draweth nigh unto me with their mouth, and honoureth me with their lips; but their heart is far from me” | Isa 29:13 | Against the tradition of the elders; follows the Septuagint wording | Matt 15:8–9 (Mk 7:6–7)
- “By hearing ye shall hear, and shall not understand...” | Isa 6:9–10 | His stated reason for parables — Isaiah’s call-vision commission fulfilled in the crowds | Matt 13:14–15 (Mk 4:12; Jn 12:40 cited by John)
- “Have ye not read... he which made them at the beginning made them male and female... they twain shall be one flesh” | Gen 1:27; 2:24 | Two creation texts joined against divorce-at-will — “what God hath joined” | Matt 19:4–5 (Mk 10:6–8)
- “Honour thy father and thy mother” | Ex 20:12; 21:17 | Both command and sanction quoted against the Corban evasion | Mark 7:10 (Matt 15:4)
- “Thou shalt do no murder... Honour thy father and thy mother... Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself” | Ex 20:12–16; Lev 19:18 | To the rich young ruler — the second table, sealed with Leviticus’ love command | Matt 19:18–19 (Mk 10:19; Lk 18:20)
- “Hear, O Israel; the Lord our God is one Lord: and thou shalt love the Lord thy God... thy neighbour as thyself” | Deut 6:4–5; Lev 19:18 | The two great commandments — Mark alone keeps the Shema attached | Mark 12:29–31 (Matt 22:37–39; Lk 10:27)
- “Is it not written, My house shall be called of all nations the house of prayer? but ye have made it a den of thieves” | Isa 56:7 + Jer 7:11 | A composite — Isaiah’s promise fused with Jeremiah’s temple indictment; Mark alone keeps “of all nations” | Mark 11:17 (Matt 21:13; Lk 19:46)
- “Out of the mouth of babes and sucklings thou hast perfected praise” | Ps 8:2 | Defending the children’s hosannas in the temple; follows the Septuagint’s “praise” for “strength” | Matt 21:16
- “The stone which the builders rejected, the same is become the head of the corner” | Ps 118:22–23 | Sealing the wicked-husbandmen parable — the rejected heir as the rejected stone | Matt 21:42 (Mk 12:10; Lk 20:17)
- “The LORD said unto my Lord, Sit thou on my right hand...” | Ps 110:1 | His own question to the Pharisees: David’s son who is David’s Lord | Matt 22:44 (Mk 12:36; Lk 20:42)
- “I am the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob” | Ex 3:6 | Against the Sadducees — resurrection proved from the tense of the burning-bush name: “God of the living” | Matt 22:32 (Mk 12:26; Lk 20:37)
- “Is it not written in your law, I said, Ye are gods?” | Ps 82:6 | Answering the blasphemy charge — “the scripture cannot be broken” | John 10:34–35
- “A man’s foes shall be they of his own household” | Micah 7:6 | The cost of the gospel foretold in Micah’s lament | Matt 10:35–36 (Lk 12:53)
- “The abomination of desolation, spoken of by Daniel the prophet” | Dan 9:27; 11:31 | On Olivet — the one prophet He cites by name: “whoso readeth, let him understand” | Matt 24:15 (Mk 13:14)

## Passion Week and the Cross
His final citations — the Psalms of the righteous sufferer become His own voice.

- “It is written, I will smite the shepherd, and the sheep shall be scattered” | Zech 13:7 | Zechariah’s “Smite” (a command) becomes “I will smite” — the Father named as the smiter | Matt 26:31 (Mk 14:27)
- “He that eateth bread with me hath lifted up his heel against me” | Ps 41:9 | Of Judas, at the supper — “that the scripture may be fulfilled” | John 13:18
- “They hated me without a cause” | Ps 35:19; 69:4 | Of the world’s hatred — “written in their law” | John 15:25
- “And he was reckoned among the transgressors” | Isa 53:12 | The one place He quotes Isaiah 53 of Himself: “this that is written must yet be accomplished in me” | Luke 22:37
- “Ye shall see the Son of man sitting on the right hand of power, and coming in the clouds of heaven” | Ps 110:1 + Dan 7:13 | Before Caiaphas, under oath — two enthronement texts fused; the court called it blasphemy | Matt 26:64 (Mk 14:62)
- “Then shall they begin to say to the mountains, Fall on us; and to the hills, Cover us” | Hos 10:8 | To the daughters of Jerusalem on the way to Golgotha | Luke 23:30
- “Eloi, Eloi, lama sabachthani? ... My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me?” | Ps 22:1 | The fourth word from the cross — cried in Aramaic; Psalm 22 ends in vindication | Matt 27:46 (Mk 15:34)
- “Father, into thy hands I commend my spirit” | Ps 31:5 | The last word in Luke — the psalmist’s evening prayer, with “Father” added | Luke 23:46
- “All things must be fulfilled, which were written in the law of Moses, and in the prophets, and in the psalms, concerning me” | — | The risen Lord’s summary claim on the whole Hebrew canon, on the Emmaus road and after | Luke 24:44 (24:27)
