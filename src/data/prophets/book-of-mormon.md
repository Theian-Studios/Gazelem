# Lehi

## Quotes

* "It filled my soul with exceedingly great joy" (1 Ne 8:12)
* "Come unto me, and partake of the fruit" (1 Ne 8:15)
* "The Lord hath redeemed my soul from hell; I have beheld his glory" (2 Ne 1:15)
* "I am encircled about eternally in the arms of his love" (2 Ne 1:15)
* "Inasmuch as ye shall keep my commandments ye shall prosper in the land" (2 Ne 1:20)
* "Arise from the dust, my sons, and be men" (2 Ne 1:21)
* "Awake, my sons; put on the armor of righteousness" (2 Ne 1:23)
* "Redemption cometh in and through the Holy Messiah; for he is full of grace and truth" (2 Ne 2:6)
* "It must needs be, that there is an opposition in all things" (2 Ne 2:11)
* "Adam fell that men might be; and men are, that they might have joy" (2 Ne 2:25)
* "Free to choose liberty and eternal life... or captivity and death" (2 Ne 2:27)

## Roles

* Patriarch
* Prophet
* Father

## Key Teachings

* Tree of Life
* Opposition in all things
* Agency
* Fall and Atonement
* Redemption

## Key Chapters

* 1 Nephi 1, 8, 10
* 2 Nephi 1–4

## Timeline

* Prophet in Jerusalem: "There came many prophets... and my father, Lehi... prayed unto the Lord, yea, even with all his heart, in behalf of his people" (1 Ne 1:4–5)
* Throne vision: "He saw the heavens open, and he thought he saw God sitting upon his throne" (1 Ne 1:8)
* Departure: "He left his house, and the land of his inheritance, and his gold, and his silver... and departed into the wilderness" (1 Ne 2:4)
* Dream of the Tree of Life: "I have dreamed a dream; or, in other words, I have seen a vision" (1 Ne 8:2)
* Prophesies of Messiah and olive tree scattering/gathering (1 Ne 10)
* Liahona: "He beheld upon the ground a round ball of curious workmanship" (1 Ne 16:10)
* Voyage: "We did arrive at the promised land" (1 Ne 18:23)
* Final blessings to posterity, including Jacob's doctrine chapter and blessings on Laman's children (2 Ne 1–4)
* Death: "He waxed old. And it came to pass that he died, and was buried" (2 Ne 4:12)

## Key Chapters

## Timeline

* Youth: "I, Nephi, being exceedingly young, nevertheless being large in stature, and also having great desires to know of the mysteries of God... he did visit me, and did soften my heart" (1 Ne 2:16)
* Brass plates: "The Spirit constrained me that I should kill Laban" (1 Ne 4:10); obtains the record and Zoram joins them (1 Ne 4)
* Vision: sees his father's dream, the condescension of God, and the history of nations (1 Ne 11–14)
* Broken bow: "I, Nephi, did make out of wood a bow... And I said unto my father: Whither shall I go to obtain food?" (1 Ne 16:23)
* Shipbuilding: "The Lord did show me from time to time after what manner I should work the timbers of the ship" (1 Ne 18:1)
* Bound on the ship: "The compass, which had been prepared of the Lord, did cease to work" (1 Ne 18:12)
* Separation: "We did take our tents and whatsoever things were possible for us, and did journey in the wilderness" (2 Ne 5:5); people take upon them the name of Nephi
* Temple: "I, Nephi, did build a temple... after the manner of the temple of Solomon" (2 Ne 5:16)
* Final ministry: psalm of Nephi (2 Ne 4), commentary on Isaiah (2 Ne 25), doctrine of Christ and farewell (2 Ne 31–33)

* Priest and Teacher
* Temple preacher
* Record keeper
* Younger brother

## Key Teachings

* Infinite Atonement
* Pride, riches, and chastity
* Allegory of the olive tree (Zenos)
* Covenant Israel's scattering and gathering

## Key Chapters

* 2 Nephi 6–10
* Jacob 2, 4, 5, 7

## Timeline

* Born in the wilderness during the eight-year sojourn (2 Ne 2:1)
* Beheld Christ young: "Thou hast beheld in thy youth his glory" (2 Ne 2:4)
* Consecrated: "I, Nephi, did consecrate Jacob and Joseph, that they should be priests and teachers over the land of my people" (2 Ne 5:26)

* Receives the plates from Nephi with charge to write sacred things (Jacob 1:1–4)
* Temple sermon rebuking pride and unchastity (Jacob 2–3)
* Records Zenos's allegory of the tame and wild olive trees (Jacob 5–6)
* Confounds the anti-Christ Sherem, who confesses and dies (Jacob 7:1–20)
* Passes plates to his son Enos: "Brethren, adieu" (Jacob 7:27)

## Key Chapters

* Prays for his rebellious son: "He has prayed with much faith concerning thee" (Mosiah 27:14)

# The Brother of Jared

## Quotes

* "Touch these stones, O Lord, with thy finger, and prepare them that they may shine forth in darkness" (Ether 3:4)
* "Behold, O Lord, thou canst do this" (Ether 3:5)
* "The veil was taken from off the eyes of the brother of Jared, and he saw the finger of the Lord" (Ether 3:6) — Moroni’s narration
* "I saw the finger of the Lord, and I feared lest he should smite me; for I knew not that the Lord had flesh and blood" (Ether 3:8)
* "Never has man come before me with such exceeding faith as thou hast" (Ether 3:9) — the Lord to the brother of Jared
* "I know, O Lord, that thou speakest the truth, for thou art a God of truth, and canst not lie" (Ether 3:12)
* "Behold, I am he who was prepared from the foundation of the world to redeem my people" (Ether 3:14) — the Lord to the brother of Jared
* "He could not be kept from beholding within the veil" (Ether 3:19; cf. 12:19–21) — Moroni’s narration

## Roles

* Founder of the Jaredite nation
* Intercessor
* Engineer

## Key Teachings

* Faith
* Prayer

## Key Chapters

* Ether 2–3

## Timeline

* Tower of Babel: pleads that the language of his family and friends not be confounded (Ether 1:34–37)
* Led to the great sea; four years pass, and "the Lord... chastened him because he remembered not to call upon the name of the Lord" (Ether 2:14)
* Builds barges "tight like unto a dish" and receives the problems of air and light to solve (Ether 2:16–25)
* Moltens sixteen small stones out of a rock and carries them to the mount Shelem (Ether 3:1)
* Sees the finger of the Lord, then the Lord Himself: shown "all the inhabitants of the earth" (Ether 3:6–26)
* Commanded to write and seal the vision; given the two stones/interpreters (Ether 3:22–28)
* Voyage: "Three hundred and forty and four days upon the water" (Ether 6:11)
* Warns against monarchy: "Surely this thing leadeth into captivity" (Ether 6:23)

# Moroni

## Quotes

* "I even remain alone to write the sad tale of the destruction of my people" (Mormon 8:3)
* "I write unto you as if ye were present, and yet ye are not. But behold, Jesus Christ hath shown you unto me, and I know your doing" (Mormon 8:35)
* "Faith is things which are hoped for and not seen" (Ether 12:6)
* "Dispute not because ye see not, for ye receive no witness until after the trial of your faith" (Ether 12:6)
* "If men come unto me I will show unto them their weakness" (Ether 12:27)
* "My grace is sufficient for all men that humble themselves before me" (Ether 12:27)
* "Then will I make weak things become strong unto them" (Ether 12:27)
* "I have seen Jesus, and... he hath talked with me face to face" (Ether 12:39)
* "Ask God, the Eternal Father, in the name of Christ, if these things are not true" (Moroni 10:4)
* "By the power of the Holy Ghost ye may know the truth of all things" (Moroni 10:5)
* "Deny not the gifts of God, for they are many" (Moroni 10:8)
* "Come unto Christ, and be perfected in him, and deny yourselves of all ungodliness" (Moroni 10:32)

## Roles

* Last record keeper and sealer of the plates
* Military commander
* Angel of the Restoration

## Key Teachings

* Faith
* Grace
* Gifts of the Spirit
* Receiving Revelation

## Key Chapters

* Mormon 8–9
* Ether 12
* Moroni 7–8, 10

## Timeline

* Commands ten thousand at Cumorah (Mormon 6:12)
* Alone: "My father hath been slain in battle... and I remain alone" (Mormon 8:3, 5)
* Hunted survivor: "I make not myself known to the Lamanites lest they should destroy me" (Moroni 1:1)
* Finishes his father's record; prophesies of the record coming forth "out of the earth" (Mormon 8:16, 26)
* Abridges the twenty-four plates of Ether; writes his discourse on faith (Ether 12)
* Writes "a few more things" — ordinances, sacrament prayers, church procedure (Moroni 1–6)
* Preserves Mormon's sermon and epistles (Moroni 7–9)
* Seals the record with his promise and farewell, more than 420 years after Christ's birth (Moroni 10:1, 34)
* Returns as a resurrected messenger to Joseph Smith, September 1823 (JS—H 1:30–33)
