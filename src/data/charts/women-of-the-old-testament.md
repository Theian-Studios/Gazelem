# Women of the Old Testament
* subtitle: The mothers, deliverers, prophetesses, and queens — at a glance
* volume: old-testament
* order: 8
* columns: The Woman | Who She Was | Remembered For | Reference (KJV)
* widths: 1 | 1.4 | 1.8 | 1

The women of the Old Testament are easy to lose in a narrative that names kings and counts armies — yet the story turns on them again and again. A midwife’s courage, a queen’s risk, a widow’s last meal, a mother’s vow: the covenant line runs straight through them. This chart gathers the most consequential, by the role each played in the unfolding story.

They are a varied company — matriarchs and Moabite converts, judges and handmaids, the faithful and (a few) the ruinous. Where a woman is remembered chiefly as a warning rather than an example, the entry says so plainly. References point to the fullest account of each.

Twenty-one women in four groups — a representative gathering, not a census. The Old Testament names far more, and leaves many unnamed (the wise woman of Tekoah, the medium of Endor, Pharaoh’s daughter). Placement reflects each woman’s chief role in the narrative; several could stand in more than one group. Rahab and Ruth, both outsiders brought into the covenant, appear by name in the genealogy of Jesus Christ (Matthew 1:5).

## The Matriarchs
The mothers of the covenant line.

- Eve | The first woman | “The mother of all living”; her choice in Eden | Gen 3:20; Moses 5:11
- Sarah | Wife of Abraham | Mother of Isaac in old age; laughed at the promise | Gen 17:15–19; 21:1–7
- Hagar | Sarah’s Egyptian handmaid | Mother of Ishmael; met by the angel at the well | Gen 16; 21:14–19
- Rebekah | Wife of Isaac | Drew water for the servant; secured the blessing for Jacob | Gen 24; 27
- Leah | First wife of Jacob | Mother of six tribes, including Judah and Levi | Gen 29:31–35
- Rachel | Beloved wife of Jacob | Mother of Joseph and Benjamin; died at Benjamin’s birth | Gen 29–30; 35:16–19

## Deliverers, Judges, and Queens
Women through whom the people were saved.

- Miriam | Sister of Moses and Aaron | Watched the ark of bulrushes; led the women in song | Ex 2:4–8; 15:20–21
- Deborah | Prophetess and judge of Israel | Summoned Barak; her victory song over Sisera | Judg 4–5
- Jael | Wife of Heber the Kenite | Slew the fleeing captain Sisera with a tent peg | Judg 4:17–22
- Ruth | A Moabite convert | “Thy people shall be my people”; great-grandmother of David | Ruth 1–4
- Abigail | Wife of Nabal, later of David | Her wisdom turned away David’s wrath | 1 Sam 25
- Esther | Queen of Persia | Risked her life to save her people: “if I perish, I perish” | Esther 4–7

## Prophetesses, Mothers, and Women of Faith
Quieter lives on which much turned.

- Jochebed | Mother of Moses | Hid her son, then nursed him for Pharaoh’s daughter | Ex 2:1–10; 6:20
- Rahab | A woman of Jericho | Hid the spies; spared, and brought into the lineage of Christ | Josh 2; 6:25; Matt 1:5
- Hannah | Mother of Samuel | Prayed for a son and gave him to the Lord; her song of praise | 1 Sam 1–2
- The widow of Zarephath | A Gentile of Sidon | Fed Elijah from her last meal; her son raised to life | 1 Kgs 17:8–24
- The Shunammite woman | A woman of substance | Built a room for Elisha; her son restored from death | 2 Kgs 4:8–37
- Huldah | A prophetess in Jerusalem | Consulted by King Josiah; confirmed the book of the law | 2 Kgs 22:14–20

## The Cautionary Figures
Women the record holds up as warnings.

- Lot’s wife | Fled Sodom with her husband | Looked back, and became a pillar of salt | Gen 19:26
- Delilah | A woman of the valley of Sorek | Coaxed out Samson’s secret and betrayed him | Judg 16:4–21
- Jezebel | Queen of Israel, wife of Ahab | Spread Baal worship; slew the prophets; her violent end | 1 Kgs 16:31; 21; 2 Kgs 9
