# Year and Place of Each Section of the Doctrine and Covenants

Years and places follow the section headings in the current edition. Unlike
the Book of Mormon's dates, which are approximate and reconstructed, these are
matters of record: each section was given on a known day in a known place, and
the places are half the story — the revelations move from a farmhouse in
Harmony to Kirtland, to Missouri, to Liberty Jail, to Nauvoo, and at last to
Salt Lake City.
