# Old Testament — Related Chapters

A selective cross-reference list for the Old Testament (KJV). Only chapters with a genuinely strong thematic, doctrinal, or narrative parallel are included; chapters without a tight match are omitted. Related chapters are drawn from all five Standard Works. Every citation was verified directly against the project scripture text files.
