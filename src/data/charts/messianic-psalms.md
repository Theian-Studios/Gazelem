# The Messianic Psalms
* subtitle: Where David’s songs foretold the Christ — and where the Gospels record it kept
* volume: old-testament
* order: 4
* columns: Psalm | The Foretelling | Fulfilled in Christ | Recorded (KJV)
* widths: 0.7 | 1.8 | 1.8 | 0.9

No book of the Old Testament is quoted in the New more than the Psalms, and no theme draws the quotations like the Messiah. David, a king and a prophet, wrote of his own throne and troubles in words that reached past himself to a greater Son — so that a psalm sung a thousand years before Calvary reads, in places, like an eyewitness account of it. This chart pairs each messianic passage with its fulfillment in Christ.

The Passion psalms are the most striking: Psalm 22 opens with the very cry from the cross and goes on to describe pierced hands, mocking crowds, and soldiers gambling for a garment — centuries before crucifixion was practiced. The apostles read these psalms as prophecy, and cited them as proof; the references below are drawn from their own use.

Twenty-one passages in four movements. Many of these psalms had a first, lesser reference to David or the reigning king; the New Testament writers saw in them a fuller sense reaching to Christ — a pattern the risen Lord Himself taught from “the psalms” on the road to Emmaus (Luke 24:44). References in the final column are the apostolic citations, so the connection is scripture’s own. A companion chart, The Categories of the Psalms, places these among the other kinds of psalm.

## The Son and His Coming
The Messiah declared, anointed, and heralded.

- Ps 2:7 | “Thou art my Son; this day have I begotten thee” | Declared the Son of God | Acts 13:33; Heb 1:5; 5:5
- Ps 8:2 | “Out of the mouth of babes… strength” | Children’s praise in the temple | Matt 21:16
- Ps 40:6–8 | “A body hast thou prepared me… I come to do thy will” | The incarnation and obedience of the Son | Heb 10:5–9
- Ps 45:6–7 | “Thy throne, O God, is for ever and ever” | The Son’s eternal throne | Heb 1:8–9
- Ps 118:26 | “Blessed be he that cometh in the name of the Lord” | Hailed at the triumphal entry | Matt 21:9

## The Ministry and the Rejection
Zeal, parables, betrayal, and the refused stone.

- Ps 69:9 | “The zeal of thine house hath eaten me up” | Cleansing the temple | John 2:17
- Ps 78:2 | “I will open my mouth in a parable” | Teaching the multitudes in parables | Matt 13:34–35
- Ps 41:9 | “Mine own familiar friend… hath lifted up his heel” | Betrayed by Judas | John 13:18
- Ps 35:19; 69:4 | “They hated me without a cause” | The world’s hatred of Him | John 15:25
- Ps 118:22–23 | “The stone which the builders refused” | The rejected cornerstone | Matt 21:42; Acts 4:11; 1 Pet 2:7

## The Passion
The crucifixion, foreseen in the Psalter.

- Ps 22:1 | “My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me?” | The cry from the cross | Matt 27:46; Mark 15:34
- Ps 22:7–8 | “He trusted on the Lord… let him deliver him” | Mocked by the rulers and passers-by | Matt 27:39–43
- Ps 22:16 | “They pierced my hands and my feet” | The crucifixion | John 20:25–27; Luke 24:39–40
- Ps 22:18 | “They part my garments… cast lots” | The soldiers divide His raiment | John 19:23–24; Matt 27:35
- Ps 69:21 | “They gave me… vinegar to drink” | Offered vinegar on the cross | Matt 27:34, 48; John 19:28–29
- Ps 34:20 | “He keepeth all his bones: not one… broken” | His legs left unbroken | John 19:36
- Ps 31:5 | “Into thine hand I commit my spirit” | His last words | Luke 23:46

## The Resurrection and the Reign
Beyond the grave — to the right hand of God.

- Ps 16:10 | “Thou wilt not leave my soul… nor suffer… corruption” | Raised from the dead | Acts 2:27, 31; 13:35
- Ps 68:18 | “Thou hast ascended on high… gifts for men” | The ascension and gifts of the Spirit | Eph 4:8
- Ps 110:1 | “Sit thou at my right hand” | Exalted to the right hand of God | Matt 22:44; Acts 2:34–35; Heb 1:13
- Ps 110:4 | “A priest for ever after the order of Melchizedek” | The eternal High Priest | Heb 5:6; 7:17, 21
