# The Commands of Jesus Christ
* subtitle: Imperatives He spoke across the four Gospels, organized by theme
* volume: new-testament
* order: 3
* columns: Command | Spoken To | Recipient | Reference (KJV)

Near-identical commands — whether repeated across Matthew, Mark, and Luke, or given on more than one occasion — are combined into a single entry, with parallel references in parentheses and audiences combined in the context column. The recipient column marks who — or what — was commanded: He addresses nature, devils, disease, and death in the same imperative mood He uses for discipleship.

79 commands across 7 themes. Where the same command appears in more than one Gospel or on more than one occasion, references and audiences are combined in a single entry. Quotations follow the King James Version; the listed reference is the primary location, with parallels in parentheses.
