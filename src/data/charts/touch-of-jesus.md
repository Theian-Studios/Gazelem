# The Touch of Jesus Christ
* subtitle: Every touch, in both directions — and what the Law said about it
* volume: new-testament
* order: 8
* columns: The Touch | Direction | The Law of Cleanness / Note | Reference (KJV)

Under the Law of Moses, the three great sources of ritual uncleanness — leprosy (Lev 13–14), the corpse (Num 19:11), and the issue of blood (Lev 15) — all transmit defilement to whoever touches them. Jesus contacts precisely these, and in every case the contagion runs backward: cleanness, life, and wholeness flow out of Him to the unclean, rather than uncleanness flowing in. The light column gives the relevant law beside each touch.

19 touches in 3 groups. The analytical spine is the reversal of Levitical contagion: where the Law expected defilement to pass to the toucher, holiness passed outward instead. Quotations follow the King James Version; the listed reference is the primary location, with parallels in parentheses.

## Reaching Toward the Unclean — Leprosy, Death, and Blood
Under the Law these three transmit uncleanness by touch. In every case the flow runs backward — cleanness passes out of Him.

- “Put forth his hand, and touched him... immediately the leprosy departed” | He touched | The leper is unclean and defiling to the touch (Lev 13–14) — yet the leper is cleansed, not the toucher defiled | Matt 8:3 (Mk 1:41; Lk 5:13)
- “He came and touched the bier” | He touched | A corpse or bier conveys uncleanness (Num 19:11) — the dead man sat up and spoke | Luke 7:14
- “He took her by the hand... and the maid arose” | He touched | Touching the dead defiles (Num 19:11) — instead, life returned to the twelve-year-old | Mark 5:41 (Matt 9:25; Lk 8:54)
- “She... touched the hem of his garment... virtue had gone out of him” | Touched by | A woman with an issue of blood is unclean, and defiles what she touches (Lev 15:25) — yet she is healed, and He calls her “daughter” | Mark 5:27–30 (Matt 9:20; Lk 8:44)

## The Hands That Healed and Blessed
The touch as the ordinary means of His mercy — and of blessing.

- “He touched her hand, and the fever left her” | He touched | Peter’s wife’s mother; she rose and ministered to them | Matt 8:15 (Mk 1:31)
- “Then touched he their eyes” | He touched | Two blind men — “according to your faith be it unto you” | Matt 9:29 (20:34)
- “He... put his fingers into his ears... and touched his tongue” | He touched | The deaf man with the impediment — “Ephphatha, Be opened” | Mark 7:33
- “He put his hands upon him... and made him look up” | He touched | The blind man of Bethsaida, healed in two stages | Mark 8:23, 25
- “He laid his hands on every one of them, and healed them” | He touched | The many brought to Him at sundown in Capernaum | Luke 4:40 (Mk 6:5)
- “He touched his ear, and healed him” | He touched | Malchus, at His own arrest — His last miracle before the cross, done for an enemy | Luke 22:51
- “He took them up in his arms, put his hands upon them, and blessed them” | He touched | The little children the disciples had rebuked | Mark 10:16 (Matt 19:15)
- “Jesus came and touched them, and said, Arise, and be not afraid” | He touched | Peter, James, and John, fallen on their faces at the Transfiguration | Matt 17:7

## Those Who Touched Him — in Faith, Love, Betrayal, and the Risen Morning
Touch running the other direction — sought, given, forbidden, and invited.

- “The whole multitude sought to touch him: for there went virtue out of him” | Touched by | The crowds pressing in Galilee — “as many as touched him were made whole” | Luke 6:19 (Mk 3:10; 6:56)
- “She... began to wash his feet with tears, and did wipe them with the hairs of her head” | Touched by | The sinful woman at Simon the Pharisee’s table — “Her sins... are forgiven” | Luke 7:38
- “Mary... anointed the feet of Jesus, and wiped his feet with her hair” | Touched by | At Bethany, six days before the passover; and the alabaster box poured on His head | John 12:3 (Mk 14:3)
- “One of his disciples, whom Jesus loved... leaning on Jesus’ bosom” | Touched by | John, at the Last Supper | John 13:23, 25
- “Judas... drew near unto Jesus to kiss him” | Touched by | The betrayer — “betrayest thou the Son of man with a kiss?” | Luke 22:47–48 (Matt 26:49)
- “Touch me not; for I am not yet ascended to my Father” | Touch withheld | Mary Magdalene at the tomb — the one touch He refused | John 20:17
- “Reach hither thy finger... reach hither thy hand, and thrust it into my side” | Touch invited | Thomas, eight days after — the touch He commanded, to answer doubt | John 20:27
