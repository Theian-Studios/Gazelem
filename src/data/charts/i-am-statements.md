# The “I Am” Statements and Self-Declarations of Jesus Christ
* subtitle: What He said of Himself — in the four Gospels, and as the risen Lord of the Restoration
* volume: new-testament
* order: 6
* columns: Declaration | Occasion & Context | Reference

This chart gathers the names Jesus claimed rather than the names He was given: the seven great Johannine metaphors, the absolute “I am” sayings that invoke the divine name of Exodus 3:14, His self-declarations in the Synoptics, and the self-namings of the risen Lord in Acts, the Book of Mormon, and the Doctrine and Covenants.

36 declarations in 4 groups. Quotations follow the King James Version and the LDS editions of the Book of Mormon and Doctrine and Covenants. In several Johannine passages the “he” of “I am he” is supplied by the translators; the Greek reads simply egō eimi, “I am.”

## The Seven “I Am” Declarations of John’s Gospel
Seven metaphors of identity, each answering a human hunger.

- “I am the bread of life” | After feeding the five thousand; to the multitude seeking loaves | John 6:35, 48
- “I am the light of the world” | In the temple, at the Feast of Tabernacles | John 8:12 (9:5)
- “I am the door of the sheep” | To the Pharisees, after healing the man born blind | John 10:7, 9
- “I am the good shepherd: the good shepherd giveth his life for the sheep” | Same discourse; the hireling contrasted | John 10:11, 14
- “I am the resurrection, and the life” | To Martha, before raising Lazarus | John 11:25
- “I am the way, the truth, and the life” | To Thomas, at the Last Supper | John 14:6
- “I am the true vine, and my Father is the husbandman” | To the Eleven, on the way to Gethsemane | John 15:1, 5

## The Absolute “I Am”
Statements with no predicate — the divine name of Exodus 3:14 claimed outright.

- “Before Abraham was, I am” | To hostile Jews in the temple; they took up stones | John 8:58
- “I that speak unto thee am he” | To the Samaritan woman — His plainest early confession of Messiahship | John 4:26
- “It is I; be not afraid” | Walking on the sea (Greek: egō eimi, “I am”) | John 6:20 (Mk 6:50; Matt 14:27)
- “If ye believe not that I am he, ye shall die in your sins” | To unbelieving Jews (“he” is supplied by the translators) | John 8:24
- “When ye have lifted up the Son of man, then shall ye know that I am he” | Foretelling the cross as the revelation of the name | John 8:28
- “That... ye may believe that I am he” | To the Twelve, foretelling the betrayal | John 13:19
- “I am he” | At the arrest — the band went backward and fell to the ground | John 18:5–6, 8
- “I am: and ye shall see the Son of man sitting on the right hand of power” | To Caiaphas, under oath — the confession that sealed His condemnation | Mark 14:62

## Self-Declarations in His Ministry
What He claimed about His person, authority, and errand.

- “The Son of man hath power on earth to forgive sins” | At the healing of the palsied man | Matt 9:6 (Mk 2:10; Lk 5:24)
- “The Son of man is Lord even of the sabbath day” | To the Pharisees, in the corn fields | Matt 12:8 (Mk 2:28; Lk 6:5)
- “In this place is one greater than the temple” | Same dispute | Matt 12:6
- “A greater than Jonas is here... a greater than Solomon is here” | To sign-seeking scribes and Pharisees | Matt 12:41–42 (Lk 11:31–32)
- “I am meek and lowly in heart” | The great invitation | Matt 11:29
- “I am among you as he that serveth” | To the Twelve, at the Last Supper | Luke 22:27
- “I am not come to destroy, but to fulfil” | On the law and the prophets | Matt 5:17
- “The Son of man came not to be ministered unto, but to minister, and to give his life a ransom for many” | To the Twelve, after James and John’s request | Matt 20:28 (Mk 10:45)
- “I am come that they might have life, and that they might have it more abundantly” | The shepherd discourse | John 10:10
- “I and my Father are one” | At the Feast of Dedication; they took up stones again | John 10:30
- “Thou sayest that I am a king. To this end was I born... that I should bear witness unto the truth” | To Pilate | John 18:37

## The Risen and Restored Lord Declares Himself
The same voice, beyond the veil of the New Testament — the Restoration’s third witness.

- “I am Jesus whom thou persecutest” | To Saul, on the Damascus road | Acts 9:5
- “I am Jesus Christ, whom the prophets testified shall come into the world... I am the light and the life of the world” | Descending at Bountiful; the multitude fell to the earth | 3 Nephi 11:10–11
- “I am the light and the life of the world. I am Alpha and Omega, the beginning and the end” | Spoken in the darkness, before His appearing | 3 Nephi 9:18
- “Behold, I am he that gave the law... the law in me is fulfilled” | To the multitude at Bountiful | 3 Nephi 15:5
- “Behold, I am the law, and the light. Look unto me, and endure to the end” | Same discourse | 3 Nephi 15:9
- “Behold, I am he who was prepared from the foundation of the world to redeem my people” | To the brother of Jared — the premortal Christ | Ether 3:14
- “I am Alpha and Omega, Christ the Lord; yea, even I am he, the beginning and the end, the Redeemer of the world” | Revelation to Martin Harris | D&C 19:1
- “Behold, I am Jesus Christ, the Son of God... the light which shineth in darkness” | To Joseph Smith and Oliver Cowdery | D&C 6:21
- “I am the first and the last; I am he who liveth, I am he who was slain; I am your advocate with the Father” | In the Kirtland Temple | D&C 110:3–4
- “Listen to the voice of Jesus Christ, your Redeemer, the Great I Am” | The covenant name reclaimed by revelation | D&C 29:1 (38:1; 39:1)
