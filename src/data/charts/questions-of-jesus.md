# The Questions of Jesus Christ
* subtitle: Questions He asked across the four Gospels, organized by theme
* volume: new-testament
* order: 1
* columns: Question | Spoken To / Context | Reference (KJV)

Near-identical questions — whether repeated across Matthew, Mark, and Luke, or asked on more than one occasion — are combined into a single entry, with the parallel references in parentheses and any distinct wordings or audiences noted beneath. Questions others asked Him are not included.

138 questions across 12 themes. Where the same question appears in more than one Gospel or on more than one occasion, the variant wordings and audiences are combined in a single entry. Quotations follow the King James Version; the listed reference is the primary location, with synoptic parallels in parentheses. Theme assignments are interpretive — many questions touch more than one theme.
