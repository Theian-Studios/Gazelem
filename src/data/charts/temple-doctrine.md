# The Development of Temple Doctrine and Practice
* subtitle: Kirtland to the present — revelation, ordinance, dedication, and the long recovery after each loss
* volume: doctrine-and-covenants
* order: 5
* form: timeline
* columns: When | What happened | Reference

The temple doctrine did not arrive whole. It came in pieces across a century — a commandment to be endowed, a house built and lost, ordinances given and then given again for the dead — and each loss was followed by a recovery that carried more than was lost.

- 2 Jan 1831 // **Revelation** | Saints commanded to Ohio to be “endowed with power from on high” | D&C 38:32
- 3 Aug 1831 // **Missouri** | Temple site dedicated at Independence, the center place of Zion | D&C 57 · D&C 58
- 27 Dec 1832 // **Olive leaf** | “Establish a house … a house of prayer”; solemn assembly commanded | D&C 88:119
- 23 Jan 1833 // **School** | School of the Prophets opens; washing of feet introduced | D&C 88:139–141
- 1 Jun 1833 // **Revelation** | Chastened for delay; Kirtland Temple dimensions revealed | D&C 95
- 23 Jul 1833 // **Kirtland** | Kirtland Temple cornerstones laid; walls rise over three years | 
- Jul–Nov 1833 // **Missouri** | Saints driven from Jackson County; Independence temple deferred | D&C 97 · D&C 101
- 21 Jan 1836 // **Ordinance** | Washings and anointings first administered in the temple attic | 
- 21 Jan 1836 // **Vision** | Vision of the celestial kingdom; salvation of the unbaptized dead | D&C 137
- 27 Mar 1836 // **Dedication** | Kirtland Temple dedicated; dedicatory prayer given by revelation | D&C 109
- 30 Mar 1836 // **Solemn assembly** | Washing of feet completes the Kirtland “endowment of power” | 
- 3 Apr 1836 // **Keys restored** | Savior accepts His house; Moses, Elias & Elijah restore sealing keys | D&C 110
- 1837–38 // **Apostasy** | Kirtland collapse; Saints depart and the temple is left behind | 
- 15 Aug 1840 // **Doctrine** | Baptism for the dead first taught at Seymour Brunson's funeral | 
- Sep 1840 // **Ordinance** | First proxy baptisms performed in the Mississippi River | 
- 19 Jan 1841 // **Revelation** | Nauvoo Temple commanded; font, washings & fulness promised | D&C 124
- 6 Apr 1841 // **Nauvoo** | Nauvoo Temple cornerstones laid at general conference | 
- 21 Nov 1841 // **Ordinance** | First baptisms for the dead in the basement font on twelve oxen | 
- 4 May 1842 // **Endowment** | Full endowment first administered to nine men, Red Brick Store | 
- Sep 1842 // **Epistles** | Recorders, witnesses, and the “welding link” between generations | D&C 127–128
- 28 May 1843 // **Sealing** | Joseph and Emma sealed for time and eternity | 
- 12 Jul 1843 // **Revelation** | Revelation on the new and everlasting covenant of marriage recorded | D&C 131–132
- 28 Sep 1843 // **Quorum** | Emma first woman endowed; the Anointed Quorum grows | 
- 27 Jun 1844 // **Martyrdom** | Joseph and Hyrum slain; the Twelve carry the temple keys forward | D&C 135
- 10 Dec 1845 // **Endowment** | Attic endowments begin; ~5,600 Saints endowed by February |

- 28 Jul 1847 // **Site chosen** | Brigham Young designates the Salt Lake Temple site, four days after arrival |
