# Book of Mormon — Primary Voice by Chapter

Rules applied:
- One primary voice per chapter (speaker or writer whose words dominate).
- Quoted prophets get credit (Isaiah, Zenos, Malachi).
- Pure narrative in the abridged books → Mormon (or Moroni in Ether/Mormon 8–9).

## Judgment calls

- **1 Ne 8 → Lehi** — first-person dream retelling dominates.
- **1 Ne 10 → Lehi** — Nephi is summarizing rather than quoting, but the prophecies are Lehi's and they are the substance of the chapter.
- **Jacob 5 → Zenos** — quoted-prophet rule, same as Isaiah.
- **Omni → Amaleki** — five writers; Amaleki wrote vv 12–30 (most of the chapter).
- **Mosiah 7 → Mormon** — Limhi speaks vv 18–33, but that's only half; narrative frames the chapter.
- **Alma 54 → Captain Moroni** — dueling epistles; Moroni's leads, Ammoron replies.
- **Hel 10 → Mormon** — the Lord speaks vv 4–11, under half the chapter.
- **3 Ne 17, 19 → Jesus Christ** — heavy narrative, but His words and acts are the substance.
- **3 Ne 30 → Jesus Christ** — two verses; v 2 is His commanded message to the Gentiles.
- **Ether 4 → Jesus Christ** — the Lord speaks vv 6–19 (14 of 19 verses).
- **Moroni 2 → Moroni** — records Christ's words to the Twelve, but Moroni credited as recorder.
