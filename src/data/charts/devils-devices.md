# The Devil’s Devices as Taught in the Book of Mormon
* subtitle: How the adversary works, in the record’s own words — deceit, ease, rage, and the captivity at the end of them
* volume: book-of-mormon
* order: 5
* columns: The device | In the record | Reference
* widths: 1.1 | 2.4 | 0.8

Four devices, each shown four ways: what the adversary claims, the passage that records it, and a note on how the device works. The order is itself an argument — he lies first, and lulls where the lie is believed; where ease will not take a soul he tries rage; and captivity is the end of all of them.

## I. He Lies
Deny God, deny hell, deny that he himself exists

- **Korihor’s confession** // “There is no God” | “But behold, the devil hath deceived me; for he appeared unto me in the form of an angel, and said unto me: Go and reclaim this people, for they have all gone astray after an unknown God. And he said unto me: There is no God; yea, and he taught me that which I should say. And I have taught his words; and I taught them because they were pleasing unto the carnal mind. // The anti-Christ’ s arguments were not his own. He was handed a script, chosen to please rather than to persuade—and it worked so well that he came to believe it himself. | Alma 30:53
- **Concealment** // “I am no devil, for there is none” | “And behold, others he flattereth away, and telleth them there is no hell; and he saith unto them: I am no devil, for there is none—and thus he whispereth in their ears, until he grasps them with his awful chains, from whence there is no deliverance. Yea, they are grasped with death, and hell; and death, and hell, and the devil, and all that have been seized therewith must stand before the throne of God, and be judged according to their works. // His most useful lie is the one told about himself. An enemy who is not believed in requires no watchfulness, no armor, and no resistance. | 2 Nephi 28:22–23
- **Sherem’s method** // Learning without God | “And he preached many things which were flattering unto the people; and this he did that he might overthrow the doctrine of Christ. And he was learned, that he had a perfect knowledge of the language of the people; wherefore, he could use much flattery, and much power of speech, according to the power of the devil. // Sherem’ s education was real and his eloquence genuine—which is the point. Nothing in the delivery betrayed the source; only the doctrine did. | Jacob 7:2, 4
- **Moroni’s measure** // The test he always fails | “For the devil is an enemy unto God, and fighteth against him continually, and inviteth and enticeth to sin, and to do that which is evil continually. But whatsoever thing persuadeth men to do evil, and believe not in Christ, and deny him, and serve not God, then ye may know with a perfect knowledge it is of the devil; for after this manner doth the devil work, for he persuadeth no man to do good, no, not one. // Moroni offers a test requiring no discernment of spirits—only attention to where a thing finally leads. | Moroni 7:12, 17
## II. He Lulls
Not a shove, but a slow and comfortable descent

- **Carnal security** // “All is well in Zion” | “And others will he pacify, and lull them away into carnal security, that they will say: All is well in Zion; yea, Zion prospereth, all is well—and thus the devil cheateth their souls, and leadeth them away carefully down to hell. Therefore, wo be unto him that is at ease in Zion! Wo be unto him that crieth: All is well! Aimed at the covenant people, not the wicked. The operative word is carefully : the descent is managed so that no alarm is ever raised. // A W H I SPER | 2 Nephi 28:21, 24–25
- **Eat, drink, and be merry** // “God will justify a little sin” | “Yea, and there shall be many which shall say: Eat, drink, and be merry, for tomorrow we die; and it shall be well with us. And there shall also be many which shall say: Eat, drink, and be merry; nevertheless, fear God—he will justify in committing a little sin… there is no harm in this… and if it so be that we are guilty, God will beat us with a few stripes, and at last we shall be saved. // God is not denied here, only downsized. The worship is kept; the obedience is quietly discounted. | 2 Nephi 28:7–8
- **Amulek’s warning** // Procrastinate the day | “I beseech of you that ye do not procrastinate the day of your repentance until the end; for after this day of life, which is given us to prepare for eternity, behold, if we do not improve our time while in this life, then cometh the night of darkness wherein there can be no labor performed. Delay needs no argument against repentance— only a reason to begin tomorrow. The device costs nothing until the window closes. // “For he seeketh that all men might be miserable like unto himself” · 2 Nephi 2:27 | Alma 34:33
- **Escalation** // The flaxen cord | “And there are also secret combinations, even as in times of old, according to the combinations of the devil, for he is the founder of all these things; yea, the founder of murder, and works of darkness; yea, and he leadeth them by the neck with a flaxen cord, until he bindeth them with his strong cords forever. A flaxen thread snaps at a touch. The trap is not the cord but the patience—each strand added while the last still seems breakable. | 2 Nephi 26:22
## III. He Enrages
Where ease will not take a soul, anger will

- **Anger against the good** // Rage in the heart | “For the kingdom of the devil must shake, and they which belong to it must needs be stirred up unto repentance, or the devil will grasp them with his everlasting chains, and they be stirred up to anger, and perish; For behold, at that day shall he rage in the hearts of the children of men, and stir them up to anger against that which is good. // Nephi sets anger directly opposite repentance. The same shaking either softens a heart or hardens it; nothing is left neutral. | 2 Nephi 28:19–20
- **The Lord’s own words** // The father of contention | “For verily, verily I say unto you, he that hath the spirit of contention is not of me, but is of the devil, who is the father of contention, and he stirreth up the hearts of men to contend with anger, one with another. Behold, this is not my doctrine, to stir up the hearts of men with anger, one against another; but this is my doctrine, that such things should be done away. // Spoken while establishing His doctrine among a people just delivered from destruction—the first thing named after baptism is the spirit that dissolves it. | 3 Nephi 11:29–30
- **Prosperity turned** // Pride and costly apparel | “For they saw and beheld with great sorrow that the people of the church began to be lifted up in the pride of their eyes, and to set their hearts upon riches and upon the vain things of the world, that they began to be scornful, one towards another, and they began to persecute those that did not believe according to their own will and pleasure. // The envyings, strife, and malice grew inside the Church—exceeding, Mormon notes, the pride of those outside it. | Alma 4:8–9
- **Zeezrom exposed** // A very subtle plan | “And thou seest that we know that thy plan was a very subtle plan, as to the subtlety of the devil, for to lie and to deceive this people that thou mightest set them against us, to revile us and to cast us out. // The aim was never to convert the hearers to error, only to turn them against the men holding the truth. Discredit the messenger and the message goes with him. | Alma 12:4
## IV. He Binds
The end of every device: captivity, then abandonment

- **Alma to Zeezrom** // The snare of the adversary | “Now this was a plan of thine adversary, and he hath exercised his power in thee. Now I would that ye should remember that what I say unto thee I say unto all. And behold I say unto you all that this was a snare of the adversary… that he might bring you into subjection unto him, that he might encircle you about with his chains, that he might chain you down to everlasting destruction. Alma names the mechanism, then deliberately widens the address: what caught one lawyer is laid for everyone. | Alma 12:5–6
- **Gadianton’s source** // Author of all sin | “Yea, it is that same being who put it into the heart of Gadianton to still carry on the work of darkness, and of secret murder; and he has brought it forth from the beginning of man even down to this time. And behold, it is he who is the author of all sin… and doth hand down their plots, and their oaths, and their covenants… from generation to generation. Mormon traces the Gadianton oaths past Nephite history to a single continuous author—handed down as fast as he can get hold upon hearts. // “ T HE DEVI L WI L L NOT S UP P OR T HI S CHI L DR EN AT T HE L AS T DAY ” · AL M A 30 :6 0 | Helaman 6:29–30
- **Moroni’s caution** // Overthrowing freedom | “For it cometh to pass that whoso buildeth it up seeketh to overthrow the freedom of all lands, nations, and countries; and it bringeth to pass the destruction of all people, for it is built up by the devil, who is the father of all lies; even that same liar who beguiled our first parents. // Moroni halts the Jaredite narrative to address a future reader directly—the one device he warns forward across time. | Ether 8:25
- **The last day** // He does not keep his own | “Ye must watch and pray always lest ye enter into temptation; for Satan desireth to have you, that he may sift you as wheat. And thus we see the end of him who perverteth the ways of the Lord; and thus we see that the devil will not support his children at the last day, but doth speedily drag them down to hell. Korihor was trampled by the very people he had persuaded. Every device terminates here: the master keeps nothing he promised. ABANDONMENT // “He persuadeth no man to do good, no, not one” · Moroni 7:17 | 3 Nephi 18:18; Alma 30:60
