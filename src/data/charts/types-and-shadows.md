# Types & Shadows of Christ
* subtitle: The Old Testament in figures — persons, sacrifices, and deliverances that pointed forward
* volume: old-testament
* order: 2
* columns: The Type | What It Foreshadows | Reference | Confirmed In
* widths: 1.2 | 2 | 1 | 1

A type is a real person, object, or event that God fashioned to prefigure a greater reality still to come. “All things which have been given of God from the beginning of the world, unto man, are the typifying of him,” Nephi wrote of Christ (2 Nephi 11:4), and the Law of Moses was “a schoolmaster to bring us unto Christ” (Galatians 3:24). This chart gathers the clearest of these figures — reading each not as an accident of history but as a rehearsal.

The final column gives the New Testament or Restoration passage that names or confirms the type, so the connection is drawn by scripture rather than by mere resemblance. Where a figure is developed most fully in latter-day revelation, that source is cited.

21 types in three groups. A type is not an allegory read back into the text but a divinely intended pattern; the New Testament writers and Book of Mormon prophets treat these figures as deliberate. Not every detail of a type corresponds to Christ — Jonah fled, Moses faltered — for the shadow is never the substance. “Now all these things happened unto them for ensamples” (1 Corinthians 10:11).

## The Persons Who Foreshadowed Him
Men whose lives traced, in part, the shape of the Redeemer.

- Adam | The head of a race; Christ the “last Adam,” head of the redeemed | Gen 2–3 | Rom 5:14; 1 Cor 15:45–47
- Melchizedek | King of Salem and priest of God Most High; the priesthood order Christ holds | Gen 14:18–20 | Heb 7:1–3; Alma 13:14–19
- Isaac | The beloved only son, offered by his father on Moriah | Gen 22:1–14 | Heb 11:17–19; Jacob 4:5
- Joseph | The beloved son, rejected and sold by his brethren, exalted to save them | Gen 37; 45:4–8 | Gen 50:20; 2 Ne 3:6–16
- Moses | Deliverer, mediator, lawgiver — “a prophet like unto me” | Deut 18:15, 18 | Acts 3:22; 3 Ne 20:23
- Jonah | Three days in the deep — the sign of the Son of Man | Jonah 1:17 | Matt 12:39–40
- Boaz | The kinsman-redeemer who buys back the lost inheritance | Ruth 4:9–10 | Lev 25:47–49; cf. Isa 59:20

## The Sacrifices and the Sanctuary
The blood, the bread, and the veil — the Atonement in ceremony.

- The Passover lamb | A lamb without blemish, its blood sparing the firstborn | Ex 12:3–13 | 1 Cor 5:7; John 1:29; 19:36
- The daily sacrifice | The Lamb slain morning and evening, continually | Ex 29:38–42 | John 1:29; Rev 13:8
- The scapegoat & Day of Atonement | The sin-bearer sent away, carrying iniquity into the wilderness | Lev 16:7–22 | Heb 9:11–14, 26–28
- The mercy seat | The place of atoning blood above the law — the propitiation | Ex 25:17–22 | Rom 3:25; Heb 9:5
- The veil of the temple | The barrier to God’s presence — “that is to say, his flesh” | Ex 26:31–33 | Heb 10:19–20; Matt 27:51
- The firstfruits sheaf | The first of the harvest waved before the Lord | Lev 23:10–11 | 1 Cor 15:20–23
- Melchizedek’s bread and wine | The emblems brought forth by the priest-king | Gen 14:18 | Matt 26:26–28

## The Deliverances and the Journey
Water, wood, and wilderness — salvation acted out on the road.

- Noah’s ark | Salvation carried safely through the waters of judgment | Gen 7:1–23 | 1 Pet 3:20–21
- Jacob’s ladder | The way between heaven and earth | Gen 28:12 | John 1:51
- The Red Sea passage | Deliverance through the water — a baptism unto Moses | Ex 14:21–31 | 1 Cor 10:1–2
- Manna | Bread rained from heaven to sustain life | Ex 16:14–15 | John 6:31–35, 48–51
- The rock at Horeb | The smitten rock pouring out living water | Ex 17:6; Num 20:11 | 1 Cor 10:4
- The brazen serpent | Lifted up, that all who look might live | Num 21:8–9 | John 3:14–15; Hel 8:14–15; Alma 33:19–22
- The cities of refuge | A refuge from the avenger for the one who flees to it | Num 35:6, 11–15 | Heb 6:18
