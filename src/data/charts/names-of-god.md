# The Names of God
* subtitle: The Hebrew names and titles of Deity in the Old Testament — and what each reveals
* volume: old-testament
* order: 1
* columns: The Name | Meaning | Reference (KJV)
* widths: 1 | 2 | 0.9

Ancient Israel knew God not by one name but by many, each opening a different facet of His character. Some are the great personal names — Elohim, Jehovah — and some are compound names coined at moments of rescue, when a man learned something new about God and marked the spot with a title. To read them together is to watch a doctrine of God assemble itself out of lived encounter.

In Latter-day Saint understanding, the name Jehovah (YHWH) is the premortal name of Jesus Christ, and Elohim is the name-title of God the Father (see Bible Dictionary, “Jehovah”; and the Father and the Son declaration of the First Presidency, 1916). The two are distinguished in latter-day scripture — “I am Jehovah, and Michael your prince” (D&C 29:11–12); Christ appeared as “Jehovah” in the Kirtland Temple (D&C 110:3–4). Meanings below follow standard Hebrew lexicons; transliterations are approximate.

Thirty-one names and titles in four groups. The reverence of later Judaism for the name YHWH led scribes to read “Adonai” in its place, and the King James translators rendered it “LORD” in small capitals — so wherever the KJV prints LORD (or GOD in small capitals), the underlying Hebrew is the sacred name Jehovah. Meanings are drawn from standard Hebrew lexicons and may be rendered variously; a few compound names (Jehovah-Raah, Jehovah-Rophe) are descriptive phrases later treasured as titles.

## The Great Personal Names
The names by which God made Himself known to the fathers.

- Elohim | God — the plural of majesty; the Creator of heaven and earth | Gen 1:1
- Jehovah (YHWH) | “I AM” — the Self-Existent, Eternal One; the covenant name | Ex 3:14–15; 6:3
- Ehyeh Asher Ehyeh | “I AM THAT I AM” — the name given to Moses at the bush | Ex 3:14
- Adonai | Lord, Master — the One who owns and rules | Gen 15:2; Deut 10:17
- Jehovah Elohim | “The LORD God” — the two great names joined | Gen 2:4
- Jah | The shortened form of Jehovah, as in “Halelu-Jah” | Ps 68:4

## The Names Formed with El
“El” — the ancient word for the Mighty One — enlarged by what He proved to be.

- El Shaddai | God Almighty — all-sufficient, the God of the patriarchs | Gen 17:1
- El Elyon | God Most High — possessor of heaven and earth | Gen 14:18–20
- El Olam | The Everlasting God — without beginning or end | Gen 21:33
- El Roi | “Thou God seest me” — the God who sees the afflicted | Gen 16:13
- El Bethel | The God of Bethel — the God of the house of God | Gen 35:7
- El Elohe Israel | “God, the God of Israel” — the name of Jacob’s altar | Gen 33:20

## The Compound Names of Jehovah
Names raised as memorials — each born of a deliverance.

- Jehovah-Jireh | “The LORD will provide” — Abraham’s word on Moriah | Gen 22:14
- Jehovah-Rophe | “The LORD that healeth thee” | Ex 15:26
- Jehovah-Nissi | “The LORD my banner” — Moses’ altar after Amalek | Ex 17:15
- Jehovah-Shalom | “The LORD is peace” — Gideon’s altar | Judg 6:24
- Jehovah-Sabaoth | “The LORD of hosts” — commander of the armies of heaven | 1 Sam 1:3; Isa 6:3
- Jehovah-Raah | “The LORD is my shepherd” | Ps 23:1
- Jehovah-Tsidkenu | “The LORD our righteousness” | Jer 23:6
- Jehovah-Shammah | “The LORD is there” — the name of the restored city | Ezek 48:35
- Jehovah-M’Kaddesh | “The LORD that doth sanctify you” | Ex 31:13; Lev 20:8

## Titles and Descriptive Names
What Israel called Him in praise and in prophecy.

- The Holy One of Israel | The set-apart God bound to His covenant people | Isa 1:4; 41:14
- The Ancient of Days | The Eternal, enthroned in judgment | Dan 7:9
- The Rock | The unmoving refuge and sure foundation | Deut 32:4; Ps 18:2
- The King of Israel | The true Sovereign of His people | Isa 44:6
- The Redeemer | The kinsman who buys His people back | Job 19:25; Isa 41:14
- The Everlasting Father | A title of the Messiah in Isaiah’s prophecy | Isa 9:6
- The First and the Last | The One who encompasses all things | Isa 44:6; 48:12
