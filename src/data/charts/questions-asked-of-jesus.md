# Questions Asked to Jesus Christ
* subtitle: What He was asked — and how He answered — across the four Gospels
* volume: new-testament
* order: 2
* columns: The Question | Asked By | How He Responded | Reference (KJV)

The mirror of the questions He asked. The fourth column is the point: adversaries almost always receive a counter-question or a refusal; disciples receive patience, object lessons, and promises; seekers are redirected from what they asked to what they needed; and at the trials the great confession is given to Caiaphas while Pilate and Herod meet near-silence. Read across the column and a pattern appears — He answers the asker, not merely the question.

45 questions in 4 groups. Requests phrased as questions (Bartimaeus, the mother of Zebedee’s sons) are included where the exchange turns on His reply. Quotations follow the King James Version; the listed reference is the primary location, with parallels in parentheses.

## The Adversaries’ Questions
Traps and challenges from Pharisees, scribes, Sadducees, Herodians, and chief priests — most answered with a counter-question.

- “Why eateth your Master with publicans and sinners?” | Pharisees, to His disciples | Proverb + scripture: “They that be whole need not a physician... I will have mercy” | Matt 9:11 (Mk 2:16; Lk 5:30)
- “Why do... thy disciples fast not?” | John’s disciples and the Pharisees | Counter-image: “Can the children of the bridechamber mourn...?” | Matt 9:14 (Mk 2:18; Lk 5:33)
- “Why do thy disciples transgress the tradition of the elders?” | Scribes and Pharisees from Jerusalem | Counter-question: “Why do ye also transgress the commandment of God by your tradition?” | Matt 15:2 (Mk 7:5)
- “Is it lawful for a man to put away his wife for every cause?” | Pharisees, tempting Him | Counter-question: “Have ye not read...?” | Matt 19:3 (Mk 10:2)
- “By what authority doest thou these things? and who gave thee this authority?” | Chief priests and elders, in the temple | Counter-question: “The baptism of John, whence was it?” | Matt 21:23 (Mk 11:28; Lk 20:2)
- “Is it lawful to give tribute unto Caesar, or not?” | Pharisees and Herodians | Counter + answer: “Whose is this image?... Render therefore unto Caesar” | Matt 22:17 (Mk 12:14; Lk 20:22)
- “Whose wife shall she be... of the seven?” | Sadducees, who deny the resurrection | Direct correction: “Ye do err, not knowing the scriptures” | Matt 22:28 (Mk 12:23; Lk 20:33)
- “Which is the great commandment in the law?” | A Pharisee lawyer, tempting Him | Direct answer: love God, and thy neighbour as thyself | Matt 22:36 (Mk 12:28)
- “Master, we would see a sign from thee” | Scribes and Pharisees | Refusal: no sign but the sign of the prophet Jonas | Matt 12:38 (16:1; Lk 11:16)
- “Is it lawful to heal on the sabbath days?” | Pharisees, watching to accuse | Counter-question: which of you would not lift a sheep from a pit? | Matt 12:10 (Mk 3:2; Lk 6:7)
- “Master, rebuke thy disciples” | Pharisees, at the triumphal entry | Refusal: “if these should hold their peace, the stones would... cry out” | Luke 19:39
- “Art thou the Christ? tell us plainly” | The Jews at the Feast of Dedication | Reproach: “I told you, and ye believed not” | John 10:24
- “Who art thou?” | The Pharisees, in the temple | “Even the same that I said unto you from the beginning” | John 8:25

## The Disciples’ Questions
The Twelve seeking understanding — met with patience, object lessons, and promises.

- “Why could not we cast him out?” | The disciples, privately | Direct: “Because of your unbelief... this kind goeth not out but by prayer and fasting” | Matt 17:19 (Mk 9:28)
- “Who is the greatest in the kingdom of heaven?” | The Twelve | Object lesson: He set a little child in the midst of them | Matt 18:1 (Mk 9:33; Lk 9:46)
- “How oft shall my brother sin against me, and I forgive him? till seven times?” | Peter | Direct + parable: “Until seventy times seven” | Matt 18:21
- “Lord, wilt thou that we command fire to come down...?” | James and John | Rebuke: “Ye know not what manner of spirit ye are of” | Luke 9:54
- “Who then can be saved?” | The disciples, astonished | Promise: “With God all things are possible” | Matt 19:25 (Mk 10:26; Lk 18:26)
- “We have forsaken all... what shall we have therefore?” | Peter | Promise of thrones + parable of the labourers | Matt 19:27
- “Grant that these my two sons may sit, the one on thy right hand...” | The mother of James and John | “Ye know not what ye ask. Are ye able to drink of the cup...?” | Matt 20:21 (Mk 10:37)
- “Lord, we know not whither thou goest; and how can we know the way?” | Thomas | “I am the way, the truth, and the life” | John 14:5
- “Lord, shew us the Father, and it sufficeth us” | Philip | “He that hath seen me hath seen the Father” | John 14:8
- “Lord, dost thou wash my feet?” | Peter | “What I do thou knowest not now; but thou shalt know hereafter” | John 13:6
- “Why speakest thou unto them in parables?” | The disciples | Direct: “Because it is given unto you to know the mysteries of the kingdom” | Matt 13:10 (Mk 4:10; Lk 8:9)
- “Master, carest thou not that we perish?” | The disciples, in the storm | He stilled the sea, then “Where is your faith?” | Mark 4:38 (Lk 8:24)
- “Lord, are there few that be saved?” | One, on the way to Jerusalem | Exhortation, not a number: “Strive to enter in at the strait gate” | Luke 13:23
- “Tell us, when shall these things be? and what shall be the sign of thy coming?” | Peter, James, John, and Andrew on Olivet | The whole Olivet discourse | Matt 24:3 (Mk 13:4; Lk 21:7)
- “Lord, wilt thou at this time restore again the kingdom to Israel?” | The apostles, after the resurrection | Deflection: “It is not for you to know the times or the seasons” | Acts 1:6

## The Seekers’ Questions
Individuals inquiring — often redirected from what they asked to what they needed.

- “Good Master, what good thing shall I do, that I may have eternal life?” | The rich young ruler | Counter-question: “Why callest thou me good?” + the commandments | Matt 19:16 (Mk 10:17; Lk 18:18)
- “Master, what shall I do to inherit eternal life?” | A lawyer, tempting Him | Counter-question: “What is written in the law? how readest thou?” | Luke 10:25
- “And who is my neighbour?” | The same lawyer | Parable of the Good Samaritan, then a counter-question | Luke 10:29
- “How can a man be born when he is old?” | Nicodemus, by night | Teaching: born of water and of the Spirit | John 3:4
- “How can these things be?” | Nicodemus | “Art thou a master of Israel, and knowest not these things?” | John 3:9
- “Art thou greater than our father Jacob...?” | The woman of Samaria | Offer of living water | John 4:12
- “Sir, give me this water, that I thirst not” | The woman of Samaria | Redirection: “Go, call thy husband” | John 4:15
- “What shall we do, that we might work the works of God?” | The crowd at Capernaum | “This is the work of God, that ye believe on him whom he hath sent” | John 6:28
- “What sign shewest thou then, that we may see, and believe thee?” | The crowd | The bread of life discourse: “I am the bread of life” | John 6:30
- “Master, speak to my brother, that he divide the inheritance with me” | One out of the company | Refusal: “Who made me a judge or a divider over you?” | Luke 12:13
- “Art thou he that should come, or do we look for another?” | John the Baptist’s two disciples | He points to His works: “the blind see... the poor have the gospel preached” | Matt 11:3 (Lk 7:19)

## Questions at the Trials
Where the great confession met Caiaphas, and near-silence met Pilate and Herod.

- “Art thou the Christ, the Son of the Blessed?” | Caiaphas, adjuring Him by oath | The great confession: “I am” | Mark 14:61 (Matt 26:63)
- “Answerest thou the high priest so?” | An officer of the court, who struck Him | “If I have spoken evil, bear witness of the evil” | John 18:22
- “Art thou the King of the Jews?” | Pilate | Counter-question then answer: “Sayest thou this thing of thyself?... Thou sayest” | John 18:33–34 (Matt 27:11; Mk 15:2)
- “What is truth?” | Pilate | No answer is recorded; he went out to the Jews | John 18:38
- “Whence art thou?” | Pilate, the more afraid | Silence, then: “Thou couldest have no power... except it were given thee from above” | John 19:9–11
- “He questioned with him in many words” | Herod, hoping to see a miracle | “He answered him nothing” | Luke 23:9
