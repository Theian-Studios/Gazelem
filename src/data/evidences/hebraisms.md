# Hebraisms
* subtitle: Hebrew literary forms in the Book of Mormon
* order: 1

Hebraisms are linguistic patterns characteristic of ancient Hebrew that appear
throughout the Book of Mormon's English text, suggesting an underlying Semitic
original. In broader biblical scholarship, such features began receiving serious
attention in the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries, as scholars increasingly
recognized that Hebrew poetry and prose followed distinctive structural
conventions unlike those of English or classical literature. Within Book of
Mormon studies, scattered observations of Hebrew-like constructions appeared in
the early twentieth century, but the watershed moment arrived in 1967, when John
W. Welch, then a missionary in Germany, recognized extended chiasmus in the Book
of Mormon. His discovery opened a field of study that has since identified
dozens of distinct Hebrew literary forms woven through the text. Many of these
patterns produce English that reads awkwardly, yet they align closely with the
grammar and idiom of biblical Hebrew, a language Joseph Smith had no exposure to
in 1829. Because much of this scholarship on Hebrew literary structure postdated
the Book of Mormon's 1830 publication, the presence of these forms stands as
compelling evidence of the book's ancient origins, pointing to authors who
thought and wrote in a Semitic tradition. For students of the text, Hebraisms
thus offer both a witness of its authenticity and an invitation to read the Book
of Mormon with the same care given to other Near Eastern scripture.

## Wordplays
* form: wordplay
* gloss: A name set beside phrasing that carries its meaning

### Jershon
* root: Hebrew YRŠ, "to inherit"; the ‑on suffix matches dozens of biblical place names
- This land Jershon is the land which we will give unto our brethren for an **inheritance** | Alma 27:22
- This will we do unto our brethren, that they may **inherit** the land Jershon | Alma 27:24
- They have lands for their **inheritance** in the land of Jershon | Alma 35:14

### Alma
* root: Hebrew ʿalm or ʿelem, "youth" or "young man"
- There was one among them whose name was Alma … And he was a **young man** | Mosiah 17:2

### Zarahemla
* root: Hebrew zeraʿ ("seed") + ḥemlah ("to have compassion, to spare")
- moved with **compassion** … let us go down to the land of Zarahemla | Alma 27:4
- We returned, those of us that were **spared**, to the land of Zarahemla | Mosiah 9:2
- Then would our brethren have been **spared**, and they would not have been burned in that great city Zarahemla | 3 Nephi 8:24

## The Wayehi Formula
* form: quotes
* gloss: Hebrew narrative advances by "and it came to pass"

Biblical Hebrew opens narrative units with wayehi, rendered in the King James
Bible as "and it came to pass." English narration omits it; the Book of Mormon
retains it throughout.

- For **it came to pass** in the commencement of the first year of the reign of Zedekiah, king of Judah | 1 Nephi 1:4
- And **it came to pass** that we went down to the land of our inheritance | 1 Nephi 3:22
- Behold, **it came to pass** that I, Enos, knowing my father that he was a just man | Enos 1:1
- And **it came to pass** that Mosiah died also, in the thirty and third year of his reign | Mosiah 29:46

## Colophons
* form: quotes
* gloss: A scribal header naming author, source and warrant

Ancient Near Eastern records open or close with a formal note identifying the
writer, the source of his authority, and the reliability of the record. The
practice is unfamiliar in English composition.

- I, Nephi, having been born of goodly parents, therefore I was taught somewhat in all the learning of my father … therefore I make a record of my proceedings in my days. | 1 Nephi 1:1 | Nephi's opening — parentage, language and warrant of accuracy
- Yea, I make a record in the language of my father, which consists of the learning of the Jews and the language of the Egyptians. | 1 Nephi 1:2
- And I know that the record which I make is true; and I make it with mine own hand; and I make it according to my knowledge. | 1 Nephi 1:3
- Now behold, I, Jarom, write a few words according to the commandment of my father, Enos, that our genealogy may be kept. | Jarom 1:1 | Author, source of commission, and purpose of the record
- And now I, Mormon, being about to deliver up the record which I have been making into the hands of my son Moroni | Words of Mormon 1:1 | An abridger's note on the transfer of the record
- I am Mormon, and a pure descendant of Lehi. | 3 Nephi 5:20 | Self-identification and lineage

## Polysyndeton
* form: phrases
* gloss: The conjunction repeated before every member of a series

Hebrew repeats the connective before each item; English style places one
conjunction before the last.

- our gold, **and** our silver, **and** our precious things | 1 Nephi 3:22
- gold, **and** silver, **and** silks, **and** scarlets, **and** fine-twined linen | 1 Nephi 13:7
- the devil, **and** death, **and** hell, **and** that lake of fire and brimstone | 2 Nephi 9:19
- thunderings, **and** lightnings, **and** earthquakes, **and** all manner of destructions | 2 Nephi 26:6
- pride, **and** wickedness, **and** abominations, **and** whoredoms | 2 Nephi 28:14
- wars, **and** contentions, **and** destructions | Enos 1:23
- his family, **and** provisions, **and** tents | 1 Nephi 2:4
- **neither** wickedness, **neither** holiness **nor** misery, **neither** good **nor** bad | 2 Nephi 2:11

## Antenantiosis
* form: phrases
* gloss: Affirming something by denying its opposite

Also called litotes. A quantity or quality is asserted by negating its
contrary, so that "not a few" means a great many.

- a man of **no small** reputation | Alma 10:4 | i.e. great repute
- they were in number **not a few** | Alma 17:34 | i.e. a great many
- and they were **not a few** | Alma 17:38 | i.e. a great many
- was **not a whit** behind him as to things pertaining to righteousness | Helaman 11:19 | i.e. fully his equal
- for he knew, **nothing doubting** | Ether 3:19 | i.e. with total certainty

## Compound Prepositions
* form: phrases
* gloss: Prepositions built from nouns, mostly parts of the body

Hebrew forms many prepositions by compounding a simple preposition with a noun
— hand, mouth, eye, sight — where English uses a single word such as by, from
or before.

- preserved **by the hand of** the Lord | 1 Nephi 5:14 | 43 occurrences
- delivered them **out of the hands of** Laban | 1 Nephi 5:8 | 30 occurrences
- spoken **by the mouth of** all the holy prophets | 1 Nephi 3:20 | 16 occurrences
- glorious **in the eyes of** the Lord | 1 Nephi 21:5 | 16 occurrences
- that thing which is great **in the sight of** God | 2 Nephi 3:24 | 15 occurrences

## Merismus
* form: phrases
* gloss: Totality expressed by naming its two poles

A whole is denoted by a pair of opposites, or by a short list standing for
everything between them.

- **black and white, bond and free, male and female** | 2 Nephi 26:33
- both **Jew and Gentile**, both **bond and free**, both **male and female** | 2 Nephi 10:16
- both **old and young**, both **bond and free**, both **male and female** | Alma 1:30
- both **old and young**, both **male and female** | 1 Nephi 8:27
- they shall come forth, both **small and great** | Mormon 9:13
- all **nations, kindreds, tongues, and people** | 1 Nephi 5:18
- the Father of **heaven and earth** | Mosiah 3:8

## Simile Curses and Covenant Oaths
* form: quotes
* gloss: A gesture performed, then applied to the one who swears

An oath is enacted physically and then turned into its own consequence — even
as this was done to the object, so let it be done to me. The form is attested
in Hittite treaty texts and at Qumran.

- the Lord should rend them even as they had rent their garments | Alma 46:21 | The rent garment
- he may cast us at the feet of our enemies, even as we have cast our garments at thy feet to be trodden under foot | Alma 46:22 | The garment trodden underfoot
- Even as this remnant of garment of my son hath been preserved, so shall a remnant of the seed of my son be preserved | Alma 46:24 | The preserved remnant
- as the Lord liveth, and as I live | 1 Nephi 4:32 | The oath formula
