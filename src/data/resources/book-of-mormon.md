## Books

### The Book of Mormon: The Earliest Text
* author: Royal Skousen
* cover: earliest-text.png
* link: Deseret Book | https://www.deseretbook.com/product/5036306.html

The Yale critical text, reconstructing the words as originally dictated; the
definitive foundation for any textual study.

### Understanding the Book of Mormon: A Reader's Guide
* author: Grant Hardy
* cover: understanding-the-book-of-mormon.jpg
* link: Deseret Book | https://www.deseretbook.com/product/5046290.html

The landmark literary reading that treats Nephi, Mormon, and Moroni as distinct
narrators with their own voices and agendas.

### The Annotated Book of Mormon
* author: Grant Hardy
* cover: annotated-book-of-mormon.png
* link: Deseret Book | https://www.deseretbook.com/product/6026925.html

Hardy's mature synthesis: the full text with scholarly annotations, essays, and
apparatus in one volume.

### Opening the Heavens
* author: John W. Welch, ed.
* cover: opening-the-heavens.png
* link: Deseret Book | https://www.deseretbook.com/product/5085862.html

A documentary collection of firsthand accounts of the foundational events of the
Restoration, including the translation process.

### Charting the Book of Mormon
* author: John W. Welch & J. Gregory Welch
* cover: charting-the-book-of-mormon.jpg
* link: Amazon | https://www.amazon.com/Charting-Book-Mormon-Personal-Teaching/dp/0934893403

Hundreds of visual charts mapping the book's structures, chronologies,
doctrines, and literary patterns. *(Out of print at Deseret Book.)*

### Christ and the New Covenant
* author: Jeffrey R. Holland
* cover: christ-and-the-new-covenant.png
* link: Deseret Book | https://www.deseretbook.com/product/4963479.html

An apostle's devotional-doctrinal treatment of the Book of Mormon's witness of
Christ; warm, literary, and deeply felt.

### By the Hand of Mormon
* author: Terryl Givens
* cover: by-the-hand-of-mormon.png
* link: Deseret Book | https://www.deseretbook.com/product/4652493.html

A reception history tracing how the Book of Mormon has functioned as a religious

### An Approach to the Book of Mormon
* author: Hugh Nibley
* cover: an-approach-to-the-book-of-mormon.jpg

### A Case for the Book of Mormon

## Articles: Text and Translation

### How Joseph Smith Translated the Book of Mormon: Evidence from the Original Manuscript
* author: Royal Skousen
* detail: JBMS 7/1, 1998
* link: Read at BYU ScholarsArchive | https://scholarsarchive.byu.edu/jbms/vol7/iss1/4

The foundational article on "tight control" in the translation, arguing from
physical manuscript evidence; the seed of the entire Critical Text Project's
conclusions.

### Timing the Translation of the Book of Mormon: "Days [and Hours] Never to Be Forgotten"
* author: John W. Welch
* detail: BYU Studies 57/4, 2018
* link: Read at BYU Studies | https://byustudies.byu.edu/article/timing-the-translation-of-the-book-of-mormon-days-and-hours-never-to-be-forgotten

His definitive day-by-day reconstruction of the roughly 65-working-day
translation, anchored to five fixed dates.

### Is the Book of Mormon a Pseudo-Archaic Text?
* author: Stanford Carmack
* detail: Interpreter 28, 2018
* link: Read the PDF | https://archive.bookofmormoncentral.org/sites/default/files/archive-files/pdf/carmack/2018-03-16/stanford_carmack_is_the_book_of_mormon_a_pseudo-archaic_text_2018.pdf

The best entry point into the Skousen–Carmack Early Modern English findings,
testing the text's syntax against twelve pseudo-biblical works.

## Articles: Literary and Linguistic

### Chiasmus in the Book of Mormon
* author: John W. Welch
* detail: BYU Studies 10/1, 1969
* link: Read at BYU Studies | https://byustudies.byu.edu/article/chiasmus-in-the-book-of-mormon

The article that started modern Book of Mormon scholarship: a missionary's
discovery of extended Hebrew inverted parallelism in Mosiah and Alma 36.

### Hebraisms in the Book of Mormon: A Preliminary Survey
* author: John A. Tvedtnes
* detail: BYU Studies 11/1, 1970
* link: Read at BYU Studies | https://byustudies.byu.edu/article/hebraisms-in-the-book-of-mormon-a-preliminary-survey

The classic catalog of Hebrew idioms, construct states, and syntax surviving in
the English text.

### On Verifying Wordprint Studies: Book of Mormon Authorship
* author: John L. Hilton
* detail: BYU Studies 30/3, 1990
* link: Read at BYU ScholarsArchive | https://scholarsarchive.byu.edu/byusq/vol30/iss3/15

The rigorous Berkeley-group stylometry study concluding that single authorship of
the Nephi and Alma texts is statistically indefensible.

## Articles: History and Geography

### When Lehi's Party Arrived in the Land, Did They Find Others There?
* author: John L. Sorenson
* detail: JBMS 1/1, 1992
* link: Read at BYU ScholarsArchive | https://scholarsarchive.byu.edu/jbms/vol1/iss1/2

The influential demographic argument that the text itself implies pre-existing
populations; reshaped how scholars read Nephite and Lamanite population history.

## Articles: Ensign and General Conference Classics

### The Book of Mormon—Keystone of Our Religion
* author: Ezra Taft Benson
* detail: Ensign, Nov. 1986
* link: Read at churchofjesuschrist.org | https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/1986/10/the-book-of-mormon-keystone-of-our-religion?lang=eng

The defining prophetic statement on the Book of Mormon of the last half-century:
the keystone of witness, doctrine, and testimony, with the promise of power
flowing into the lives of serious students.

### Safety for the Soul
* author: Jeffrey R. Holland
* detail: Ensign, Nov. 2009
* link: Read at churchofjesuschrist.org | https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/2009/10/safety-for-the-soul?lang=eng

Perhaps the most forceful apostolic testimony of the book ever given over the
pulpit, built around Hyrum's reading of Ether 12 at Carthage; the natural
companion to *Christ and the New Covenant*.

### The Book of Mormon: What Would Your Life Be Like without It?
* author: Russell M. Nelson
* detail: Ensign, Nov. 2017
* link: Read at churchofjesuschrist.org | https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/2017/10/the-book-of-mormon-what-would-your-life-be-like-without-it?lang=eng

His three-question framework, with the appended lists of what the book is,
affirms, refutes, fulfills, clarifies, and reveals.

### Mounting Evidence for the Book of Mormon
* author: Daniel C. Peterson
* detail: Ensign, Jan. 2000
* link: Read at churchofjesuschrist.org | https://www.lds.org/ensign/2000/01/mounting-evidence-for-the-book-of-mormon?lang=eng

The best single Ensign survey of the scholarly evidences: Nahom, Hebraisms,
wordprints, Mesoamerican correlations, all in an accessible magazine treatment.

## Primary Sources and Research Tools

### Printer's Manuscript of the Book of Mormon
* author: Joseph Smith Papers
* link: Explore at Joseph Smith Papers | https://www.josephsmithpapers.org/articles/revelations-volume-3-printers-manuscript-of-the-book-of-mormon

Full-color photographs of every page with Skousen and Jensen's color-coded
transcript distinguishing each scribe's hand; the original manuscript fragments,
including the 2017 multispectral images, are on the same site.

### The Book of Mormon Critical Text Project
* link: Explore the site | https://criticaltext.byustudies.byu.edu/translating-book-mormon-evidence-original-manuscript

The online home of Skousen's project at BYU Studies, with papers, updates, and
supporting materials for all the volumes.

## Study Websites

### Scripture Central
* link: Sample KnoWhy | https://scripturecentral.org/knowhy/why-is-the-presence-of-chiasmus-in-the-book-of-mormon-significant
* link: Evidence database | https://scripturecentral.org/evidence/archaic-vocabulary

The biggest hub for accessible Book of Mormon scholarship. The KnoWhy series
offers hundreds of short, sourced studies keyed to specific verses, and the
Evidence database catalogs individual evidences with full bibliographies.

### Scripture Central Archive
* link: Browse the archive | https://archive.bookofmormoncentral.org/content/when-lehis-party-arrived-land-did-they-find-others-there

The digitized FARMS and Maxwell Institute corpus: decades of JBMS, *Insights*,
FARMS Review, conference papers, and out-of-print books, all searchable and
free.

### Maxwell Institute
* link: Visit the Maxwell Institute | https://mi.byu.edu/tag/john-w-welch

BYU's Neal A. Maxwell Institute for Religious Scholarship: current scholarship,
the twelve-volume Brief Theological Introductions series — one short theological
reading per book of the Book of Mormon — and recorded lectures, including the
annual Willes Center Book of Mormon Lecture series.

### BYU Studies
* link: Visit BYU Studies | https://byustudies.byu.edu/article/chiasmus-in-the-book-of-mormon

Sixty-plus years of peer-reviewed scholarship on the Book of Mormon and
Latter-day Saint studies generally, from Welch's original 1969 chiasmus article
to current issues. Also hosts free online books, including the full second
edition of *Opening the Heavens* with its 200+ translation source documents.

### BYU Religious Studies Center
* link: Visit the Religious Studies Center | https://rsc.byu.edu/vol-16-no-2-2015/gospel-according-nephi-essay-2-nephi-31

Hundreds of free books and *Religious Educator* articles from BYU's religion
faculty, including full Sperry Symposium volumes and titles like *The Coming
Forth of the Book of Mormon* — a deep well of readable, faithful scholarship for
both study and teaching prep.
