# Calendar Years of the Book of Mormon, Chapter by Chapter

Dates follow the traditional chronology printed in the LDS edition date footers, anchored to two pegs: Lehi's departure from Jerusalem at **c. 600 B.C.** (1 Ne 10:4; 19:8) and the beginning of the reign of the judges at **c. 91 B.C.** (Nephite judge-year N ≈ 92 − N B.C.). After the sign of Christ's birth, years are counted from **A.D. 1**. All dates are approximate; ranges reflect either the span of events in the chapter or the uncertainty of the footers.

## Ether

The Jaredite record carries no internal calendar dates; the traditional framing runs from the great tower (conventionally placed well before 2000 B.C.) to the Jaredite destruction sometime before the people of Zarahemla found Coriantumr (Omni 1:21), plausibly in the range of c. 600–300 B.C. Moroni made the abridgment c. A.D. 400–421.
