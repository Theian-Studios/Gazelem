# The Metaphors of Paul
* subtitle: One gospel spoken in five worlds — body, building, arena, army, and field
* volume: new-testament
* order: 10
* columns: The Image | What It Pictures | The Doctrine It Carries | Reference (KJV)
* widths: 1.2 | 1.6 | 1.8 | 1

Paul’s images cluster in five domains, and sorting them reveals a missionary at work: he translates a single gospel into the vocabularies of five audiences — the physician’s body, the mason’s building, the athlete’s arena, the soldier’s campaign, and the farmer’s, family’s, and merchant’s everyday world. Each image is chosen to carry a doctrine.

31 images in 5 groups. Several passages blend domains — 1 Corinthians 3:9 calls the church both “God’s husbandry” and “God’s building” in one breath, and Ephesians 2 grows a building like a body — which is the point: Paul’s metaphors overlap because one reality is being described from many angles. Quotations follow the King James Version.

## The Body
The church as a living organism — many members, one life, one Head.

- “For as the body is one, and hath many members... so also is Christ” | The many-membered congregation | No member is dispensable; diversity serves unity | 1 Cor 12:12
- “Now ye are the body of Christ, and members in particular” | The Corinthians in their variety of gifts | The believers together are Christ’s corporeal presence | 1 Cor 12:27
- “He is the head of the body, the church” | Christ over the whole | Headship, life, and direction flow from Him | Col 1:18 (Eph 1:22–23)
- “That there should be no schism in the body” | The suffering of one part felt by all | Mutual care; the honour of the weaker member | 1 Cor 12:25
- “Whom the whole body fitly joined together... maketh increase of the body” | A body growing into maturity | Every joint supplies; the church builds itself in love | Eph 4:16
- “We... are one body in Christ, and every one members one of another” | The Roman believers | Belonging to Christ means belonging to each other | Rom 12:5

## The Building and the Temple
The people of God as a rising structure on one foundation.

- “Ye are God’s building” | The Corinthians as construction in progress | God is the builder; the church is His edifice | 1 Cor 3:9
- “Other foundation can no man lay... which is Jesus Christ” | The one footing beneath all | Christ alone is the ground; build with care | 1 Cor 3:11
- “Know ye not that ye are the temple of God?” | The congregation as sanctuary | The Spirit indwells the gathered people | 1 Cor 3:16 (6:19)
- “Built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Jesus Christ himself being the chief corner stone” | Jew and Gentile joined in one house | The corner stone that aligns the whole structure | Eph 2:20
- “All the building fitly framed together groweth unto an holy temple in the Lord” | Living stones rising into a temple | A structure that grows, like a body | Eph 2:21
- “Rooted and built up in him, and stablished in the faith” | The Colossians grounded in Christ | Foundation and growth held in one image | Col 2:7

## The Athletic Arena
The Christian life as a race to be run and a contest to be won.

- “They which run in a race run all, but one receiveth the prize? So run, that ye may obtain” | The stadium at Corinth’s Isthmian games | Run to win; discipline the body for the crown | 1 Cor 9:24
- “Now they do it to obtain a corruptible crown; but we an incorruptible” | The fading victor’s wreath | An imperishable prize is worth every sacrifice | 1 Cor 9:25
- “So fight I, not as one that beateth the air” | The boxer who does not shadow-box | Purposeful effort, not aimless striving | 1 Cor 9:26
- “I press toward the mark for the prize of the high calling of God” | The runner leaning for the finish line | Forgetting what is behind; straining forward | Phil 3:14
- “If a man also strive for masteries, yet is he not crowned, except he strive lawfully” | The athlete bound by the rules | No reward without lawful striving | 2 Tim 2:5
- “I have fought a good fight, I have finished my course, I have kept the faith” | Paul at the end of the race | The crown of righteousness laid up | 2 Tim 4:7–8

## The Soldier and the Warfare
The believer armed for a spiritual, not carnal, campaign.

- “Put on the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil” | The soldier arming for battle | Stand equipped; the fight is against unseen powers | Eph 6:11
- “The shield of faith... the helmet of salvation... the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God” | The full panoply, piece by piece | Every defence is a gift; the one weapon is the word | Eph 6:16–17
- “Thou therefore endure hardness, as a good soldier of Jesus Christ” | Timothy called to soldierly endurance | Suffering is part of the service | 2 Tim 2:3
- “No man that warreth entangleth himself with the affairs of this life” | The soldier free of civilian distraction | Singleness of purpose; please the commander | 2 Tim 2:4
- “The weapons of our warfare are not carnal, but mighty through God” | Siege engines that pull down strong holds | The battle is fought with divine, not fleshly, power | 2 Cor 10:4
- “Epaphroditus, my brother, and companion in labour, and fellowsoldier” | A comrade in the campaign | Ministry as shared soldiering (also Archippus, Philem 1:2) | Phil 2:25

## The Field, the Household, and the Marketplace
Images from farming, family, and commerce — the everyday world made a parable of grace.

- “I have planted, Apollos watered; but God gave the increase” | Farmhands over one field | Ministers labour; only God makes it grow | 1 Cor 3:6
- “Thou, being a wild olive tree, wert graffed in among them” | Gentiles grafted into Israel’s tree | Salvation by ingrafting, not by nature — stand by faith | Rom 11:17
- “Whatsoever a man soweth, that shall he also reap” | Seedtime and harvest | A moral law as certain as an agricultural one | Gal 6:7
- “Ye have received the Spirit of adoption, whereby we cry, Abba, Father” | A child taken legally into the family | Not slaves but sons and heirs | Rom 8:15
- “The earnest of the Spirit in our hearts” | A deposit, a down payment (Greek arrabōn) | The Spirit as God’s pledge of the full inheritance | 2 Cor 1:22 (5:5; Eph 1:14)
- “Ye were sealed with that holy Spirit of promise” | A seal of ownership and guarantee | Marked as God’s until the redemption of the purchase | Eph 1:13
- “The law was our schoolmaster to bring us unto Christ” | The household tutor (Greek paidagōgos) | The law escorts, then hands the child to Christ | Gal 3:24
