# Grace and Works in Paul
* subtitle: Both, in the same hand — a T-chart of the New Testament’s both/and
* volume: new-testament
* order: 11
* columns: Grace — Salvation as Gift | The Two Held Together | Works — Judged by Deeds
* widths: 1 | 1 | 1

Paul is quoted on one side to prove salvation is by grace, on the other to prove it is by works — usually by readers holding only half of him. Here both bodies of his own language face each other, with the passages that bind them together set between. James stands in the works column as Paul’s complement, not his opponent.

19 passages. The outer columns stand in tension; the centre shows Paul resolving it himself — most pointedly in Ephesians 2:10, the verse right after “not of works, lest any man should boast.” Quotations follow the KJV.

- “For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God: Not of works, lest any man should boast” // **Eph 2:8–9** *· a gift received, not a wage earned* | “For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus unto good works, which God hath before ordained that we should walk in them” // **Eph 2:10** *· the verse right after “not of works”: saved by grace, created for works* | “Who will render to every man according to his deeds” // **Rom 2:6** *· judgment measured by deeds*
- “Being justified freely by his grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus” // **Rom 3:24** *· justified “freely,” grounded in the Atonement* | “In Jesus Christ neither circumcision availeth any thing... but faith which worketh by love” // **Gal 5:6** *· the faith that saves is a faith that works* | “Not the hearers of the law are just before God, but the doers of the law shall be justified” // **Rom 2:13** *· doers, not hearers, are justified*
- “To him that worketh not, but believeth on him that justifieth the ungodly, his faith is counted for righteousness” // **Rom 4:5** *· righteousness credited to faith* | “Work out your own salvation... For it is God which worketh in you both to will and to do” // **Phil 2:12–13** *· we work because God works in us — both at once* | “To them who by patient continuance in well doing seek for glory and honour... eternal life” // **Rom 2:7** *· eternal life sought by persevering in well-doing*
- “If by grace, then is it no more of works: otherwise grace is no more grace” // **Rom 11:6** *· grace and merit, at root, are opposites* | “That being justified by his grace... they which have believed in God might be careful to maintain good works” // **Titus 3:7–8** *· justified by grace, therefore diligent in good works* | “We must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ; that every one may receive the things done in his body” // **2 Cor 5:10** *· a judgment of works awaits every believer*
- “Not by works of righteousness which we have done, but according to his mercy he saved us” // **Titus 3:5** *· mercy, not our righteousness, saves* |  | “The fire shall try every man’s work of what sort it is” // **1 Cor 3:13** *· each one’s work is tested for its quality*
- “Who hath saved us... not according to our works, but according to his own purpose and grace” // **2 Tim 1:9** *· God’s grace precedes our works* |  | “God is not mocked: for whatsoever a man soweth, that shall he also reap” // **Gal 6:7** *· a moral harvest follows moral sowing*
- “Therefore we conclude that a man is justified by faith without the deeds of the law” // **Rom 3:28 (Gal 2:16)** *· the law of Moses does not justify* |  | “Let us not be weary in well doing: for in due season we shall reap, if we faint not” // **Gal 6:9** *· the harvest comes to those who persevere*
-  |  | “Faith, if it hath not works, is dead... by works a man is justified, and not by faith only” // **James 2:17, 24** *· James: a living faith works*
