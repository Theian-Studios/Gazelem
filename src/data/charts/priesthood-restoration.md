# The Restoration of Priesthood and Keys
* subtitle: Harmony to Salt Lake City, 1829–1978 — angelic conferral, offices organized, and the keys committed
* volume: doctrine-and-covenants
* order: 4
* form: timeline
* columns: When | What happened | Reference

Three threads run together here: priesthood conferred by angels, offices and quorums organized among men, and the temple keys committed. The chronology is the argument — the authority came before the organization, and the keys before the temple that used them.

- 15 May 1829 // **Aaronic keys** | John the Baptist confers the Priesthood of Aaron on Joseph and Oliver | D&C 13; JS—H 1:68–72
- May–Jun 1829 // **Apostolic keys** | Peter, James, and John confer the Melchizedek Priesthood and apostleship | D&C 27:12; 128:20
- 6 Apr 1830 // **First & Second Elders** | Church organized; offices of elder, priest, teacher, and deacon set in order | D&C 20
- 25 Jan 1832 // **President sustained** | Joseph sustained President of the High Priesthood at Amherst | 
- 22 Sep 1832 // **Oath & covenant** | Revelation on priesthood: lineage, keys, and the oath and covenant | D&C 84
- Mar 1833 // **First presidency** | Counselors ordained and given the keys of the kingdom with Joseph | D&C 90
- 18 Dec 1833 // **Patriarch** | Joseph Smith Sr. ordained Patriarch to the Church | 
- 17 Feb 1834 // **High council** | First high council organized at Kirtland | D&C 102
- Feb 1835 // **Twelve & Seventy** | Quorums of the Twelve Apostles and the Seventy called and ordained | 
- 28 Mar 1835 // **Order defined** | Revelation on priesthood: two priesthoods, quorum order, presiding keys | D&C 107
- 3 Apr 1836 // **Sealing keys** | Moses, Elias, and Elijah commit the keys of gathering, covenant, and sealing | D&C 110
- 19 Jan 1841 // **Fulness promised** | Nauvoo revelation: temple ordinances to restore the fulness of the priesthood | D&C 124:28
- 4 May 1842 // **Endowment** | Endowment of priesthood ordinances first administered, Red Brick Store | 
- 8 Jun 1978 // **Priesthood for all** | Revelation to President Kimball extends priesthood to every worthy male member | OD 2
