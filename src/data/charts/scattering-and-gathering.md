# The Scattering & Gathering of Israel
* subtitle: The two great movements of covenant history — cast off, then brought home
* volume: old-testament
* order: 10
* columns: The Prophecy | The Word Given | Reference (KJV)
* widths: 1.2 | 2.2 | 1

From Moses onward the prophets spoke of one long arc: a covenant people who, for breaking the covenant, would be scattered “among all people, from the one end of the earth even unto the other” (Deuteronomy 28:64) — and who, in the last days, would be gathered again by the same God who dispersed them. The scattering was warning and judgment; the gathering is mercy and promise. This chart sets the two side by side.

For Latter-day Saints the second movement is not yet finished. The gathering of Israel — the return of the scattered remnants to the covenant, to their lands of inheritance, and to the truth — is understood as a work now underway, its keys restored to the earth by Moses himself (D&C 110:11). The final section gathers the Restoration passages that carry the ancient promise into the present day.

Combines two related chart ideas — the scattering and the gathering — because in scripture they are one promise with two halves, spoken in the same breath (Deuteronomy 30; Isaiah 11; Ezekiel 37). References are representative; the gathering in particular is a theme so pervasive in Isaiah, Jeremiah, and Ezekiel that only landmark passages are listed. The Restoration section reflects Latter-day Saint scripture and teaching that the gathering is both spiritual (unto Christ and His covenant) and, in time, literal (unto lands of inheritance).

## The Scattering Foretold
The covenant curse, and its execution by Assyria and Babylon.

- The covenant curse | Scattered among the nations, few in number, no rest for the sole of the foot | Lev 26:33; Deut 28:64–65
- Moses’ farewell | The Lord shall scatter you, and ye shall be left few | Deut 4:27
- Isaiah’s commission | Cities wasted, the land forsaken, until a remnant only remains | Isa 6:11–13
- Hosea’s sign | “Lo-ammi” — ye are not my people | Hosea 1:9
- Amos’ sieve | Israel sifted among all nations, as corn in a sieve | Amos 9:9
- Ezekiel by the river | Scattered among the heathen, dispersed through the countries | Ezek 12:15
- The fall of the North | Assyria carries Israel away out of their own land | 2 Kgs 17:6, 23
- The fall of Judah | Babylon carries Judah captive; seventy years foretold | 2 Kgs 25:11; Jer 25:11

## The Gathering Promised
The ensign, the highway, and the return from the four corners.

- Moses’ farther word | From thence will the Lord gather thee, and bring thee into the land | Deut 30:1–5
- The ensign to the nations | The Lord shall set His hand again the second time; gather from the four corners | Isa 11:11–12
- From east and west | “Fear not: for I am with thee: I will bring thy seed from the east” | Isa 43:5–6
- The nursing kings | A standard lifted to the Gentiles, who bring thy sons in their arms | Isa 49:22
- The fishers and hunters | “I will bring them again into their land,” by fishers and by hunters | Jer 16:14–16
- From the north country | Gathered from the north and the coasts of the earth | Jer 31:8–10
- The two sticks made one | Judah and Joseph joined; one nation, one king | Ezek 37:15–22
- Plucked up no more | “I will plant them upon their land, and they shall no more be pulled up” | Amos 9:14–15
