# Messianic Prophecies & Their Fulfillment
* subtitle: What the prophets foretold, and where the Gospels record it kept
* volume: old-testament
* order: 3
* columns: The Prophecy | Foretold (KJV) | The Fulfillment | Recorded (KJV)
* widths: 1.6 | 0.9 | 1.6 | 0.9

Long before Bethlehem, the Old Testament sketched the Messiah in fragments — a lineage here, a birthplace there, a manner of death set down in a psalm a thousand years early. This chart lays each foretelling beside the New Testament passage that records its keeping. Read down the first column and a portrait assembles itself out of scattered pieces; read across, and the distance between promise and fulfillment — often centuries — becomes the point.

The references follow the King James text. Some prophecies were fulfilled once and plainly (Bethlehem, the virgin birth); others the New Testament writers saw fulfilled in a fuller or typological sense than their first setting. Where a passage is applied to Christ by the apostles themselves, that application is noted.

46 prophecies in five movements. The list is representative, not exhaustive; the Old Testament holds hundreds of messianic touches, and Jesus told the disciples on the Emmaus road that “all the scriptures” testified of Him (Luke 24:27, 44). Where the New Testament writers themselves cite a passage as fulfilled, the citation is given; a few (the seed of the woman, the smitten shepherd) are read messianically by long and near-universal tradition.

## His Lineage and Coming
The narrowing line — from the seed of the woman to the throne of David.

- The seed of the woman would bruise the serpent | Gen 3:15 | God sent forth his Son, made of a woman | Gal 4:4
- In Abraham’s seed all nations blessed | Gen 22:18 | The seed is Christ; Abraham’s son | Gal 3:16; Matt 1:1
- The sceptre would not depart from Judah | Gen 49:10 | Our Lord sprang out of Juda | Heb 7:14; Rev 5:5
- Of the house and throne of David | 2 Sam 7:12–13; Isa 9:7 | The throne of his father David | Luke 1:32–33
- A messenger sent to prepare the way | Isa 40:3; Mal 3:1 | John the Baptist in the wilderness | Matt 3:1–3; Mark 1:2–4

## His Birth and Infancy
The circumstances no ordinary child could arrange.

- Born of a virgin — Immanuel | Isa 7:14 | A virgin shall bring forth a son | Matt 1:22–23
- Born in Bethlehem of Judah | Micah 5:2 | Jesus was born in Bethlehem | Matt 2:1; Luke 2:4–7
- Called out of Egypt | Hosea 11:1 | The flight to Egypt and return | Matt 2:14–15
- Weeping in Ramah — children slain | Jer 31:15 | Herod slew the children of Bethlehem | Matt 2:16–18

## His Ministry
The works and words the prophets assigned to Him.

- A prophet like unto Moses | Deut 18:15, 18 | Peter names Christ that prophet | Acts 3:20–22
- Light dawning in Galilee | Isa 9:1–2 | He dwelt in Capernaum by the sea | Matt 4:13–16
- Anointed to preach good tidings | Isa 61:1–2 | Read in the synagogue: “fulfilled” | Luke 4:17–21
- The eyes of the blind opened | Isa 35:5–6 | The blind see, the deaf hear | Matt 11:4–5
- He would speak in parables | Ps 78:2 | Without a parable spake he not | Matt 13:34–35
- Riding to Zion, lowly, upon an ass | Zech 9:9 | The triumphal entry | Matt 21:4–9; John 12:14–15
- The stone the builders refused | Ps 118:22 | The head of the corner | Matt 21:42; Acts 4:11

## His Betrayal, Trial, and Death
The Passion foretold in the Law, the Psalms, and the Prophets.

- Betrayed by a familiar friend | Ps 41:9 | Judas, who ate bread with Him | John 13:18
- Sold for thirty pieces of silver | Zech 11:12 | The chief priests weighed the price | Matt 26:15
- The silver cast to the potter | Zech 11:13 | The potter’s field bought with it | Matt 27:5–7
- The shepherd smitten, sheep scattered | Zech 13:7 | The disciples forsook Him and fled | Matt 26:31, 56
- Silent before His accusers | Isa 53:7 | He answered nothing | Matt 27:12–14
- Smitten, spat upon, scourged | Isa 50:6 | They spit on Him and struck Him | Matt 26:67; Mark 14:65
- Numbered with the transgressors | Isa 53:12 | Crucified between two thieves | Mark 15:27–28; Luke 22:37
- Hands and feet pierced | Ps 22:16 | The print of the nails | John 20:25–27; Luke 24:39
- Mocked and derided — “he trusted in God” | Ps 22:7–8 | The rulers and passers-by mocked | Matt 27:39–43
- Given vinegar and gall | Ps 69:21 | They gave Him vinegar to drink | Matt 27:34, 48; John 19:28–29
- Lots cast for His garments | Ps 22:18 | The soldiers parted His raiment | John 19:23–24; Matt 27:35
- Not a bone of Him broken | Ps 34:20; Ex 12:46 | They brake not His legs | John 19:33, 36
- His side pierced — “they shall look” | Zech 12:10 | A spear pierced His side | John 19:34, 37
- With the rich in His death | Isa 53:9 | Buried in Joseph’s new tomb | Matt 27:57–60
- Wounded for our transgressions | Isa 53:5–6 | He bare our sins in His body | 1 Pet 2:24; 2 Cor 5:21

## His Resurrection, Ascension, and Reign
What the grave could not hold, and what follows it.

- Not left in the grave, nor to see corruption | Ps 16:10 | God raised Him up | Acts 2:27, 31; 13:35
- Ascended on high, gifts to men | Ps 68:18 | The ascension | Eph 4:8; Acts 1:9
- Seated at the Lord’s right hand | Ps 110:1 | Exalted to God’s right hand | Acts 2:34–35; Heb 1:13
- A priest after the order of Melchizedek | Ps 110:4 | Our great High Priest | Heb 5:6; 6:20
- A light to the Gentiles | Isa 49:6 | Salvation to the ends of the earth | Luke 2:32; Acts 13:47
- An everlasting kingdom given the Son of Man | Dan 7:13–14 | Of His kingdom no end | Luke 1:33; Rev 11:15
